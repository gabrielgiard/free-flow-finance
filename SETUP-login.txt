SWITCHING ON THE OPTIONAL LOGIN  (about 15 minutes, free, no credit card)
=========================================================================

What this does
--------------
Visitors can sign in (with Google, or with an email and password) to keep
their portfolio, Monte Carlo settings, watchlist, Daily Ten streak and saved
"Your case" scenarios on every device. Nothing on the site needs a login.

Until you finish these steps the site simply has no "Sign in" button. The
watchlist and saved cases already work without it, saved on each device.
So you can upload the new files first and do this later.

You need: a Google account. Firebase is Google's free service for this.
Its free plan (called "Spark") never charges you; if the site ever got
enormous, sync would pause for the day rather than cost money.


STEP 1. Create the Firebase project
-----------------------------------
1. Go to  https://console.firebase.google.com  and sign in with Google.
2. Click "Create a project" (or "Get started by setting up a Firebase project").
3. Name it  freeflow-finance  and click Continue.
4. If it asks about Gemini or AI assistance, you can leave it off. Continue.
5. When it asks about Google Analytics, switch it OFF. Click "Create project".
6. Wait for it to finish, then click Continue.


STEP 2. Register the website and copy its settings
--------------------------------------------------
1. On the project's home page, click the web icon. It looks like  </>
   (it may sit under "Add app" or "Get started by adding Firebase to your app").
2. App nickname:  FreeFlow website
   Do NOT tick "Also set up Firebase Hosting".
3. Click "Register app".
4. Firebase now shows some code. Find the part that starts with

       const firebaseConfig = {

   and ends with

       };

   Select from "const" down to and including "};" and copy it.
   Paste it somewhere safe for a minute (Notes is fine).
   These values are not secret: every visitor's browser needs them.
5. Click "Continue to console".


STEP 3. Switch on the two ways to sign in
-----------------------------------------
1. In the left menu: Build  >  Authentication  >  "Get started".
2. Open the "Sign-in method" tab.
3. Click "Email/Password". Turn ON the first switch (Email/Password).
   Leave "Email link" off. Click Save.
4. Click "Add new provider", then "Google". Turn it on.
   Pick your email as the "Project support email". Click Save.


STEP 4. Allow your website's address
------------------------------------
1. Still in Authentication, open the "Settings" tab.
2. Click "Authorized domains", then "Add domain".
3. Type   free-flow-finance.pages.dev   and click Add.
   (If you later get your own domain name, add it here too.)


STEP 5. Create the database
---------------------------
1. In the left menu: Build  >  Firestore Database  >  "Create database".
2. If it asks for an edition, choose "Standard".
3. Location: leave the suggested one (for example nam5, United States).
   This can't be changed later, and the default is fine.
4. Choose "Start in production mode". Click Create.


STEP 6. Paste the security rules
--------------------------------
These rules are what keep every account private.
1. In Firestore Database, open the "Rules" tab.
2. Delete everything in the box.
3. Open the file  firestore.rules  (it's in the zip, next to README.md),
   copy ALL of it, and paste it into the box.
4. Click "Publish".


STEP 7. Put the settings into the website
-----------------------------------------
EITHER send me the block you copied in Step 2 and I'll put it in the next
zip for you,

OR do it yourself on GitHub:
1. Open your repository on github.com, go into the  docs  folder and click
   auth-config.js.
2. Click the pencil icon (Edit this file).
3. At the very bottom is the line

       const firebaseConfig = null;

   Select that whole line and paste your block over it, so the line is
   replaced. Don't paste it underneath: there must be only one
   "const firebaseConfig".
4. Click "Commit changes" (green button), then "Commit changes" again.
5. Wait about a minute for Cloudflare to publish, then open the site.
   A small round account button appears at the top right, next to search.


STEP 8. Try it
--------------
1. Click the round button, then "Create an account" with your email.
2. Star a company (the "Watch" button on any company page).
3. Open the site on your phone, sign in with the same account, and the star
   is there too.
Then in Firebase, Authentication > Users shows your account, and Firestore
Database > Data shows one record under "users".

Inside Instagram's built-in browser, Google sign-in is blocked by Google
itself. The site explains this and email sign-in still works there.


Good to know
------------
- Deleting accounts: people can delete their own account from the account
  window. You can also delete any user in Authentication > Users, and their
  record in Firestore Database > Data > users.
- The privacy page on the site (#/privacy, linked in the footer) says
  exactly what is stored. If you ever add anything else, update it.
- Optional: in Authentication > Templates you can change the sender name
  on the password reset email to "FreeFlow Finance".
- To switch the login off again, put back  const firebaseConfig = null;
  Nobody loses anything saved on their own device.
