About Deere & Company

Founded in 1837 by John Deere, a blacksmith who designed a steel plough that could cut through Midwest prairie soil. John May is CEO, headquartered in Moline, Illinois. Deere makes agricultural equipment — tractors, combines, sprayers — plus construction and forestry machinery through its Wirtgen and Hitachi partnerships. Its precision agriculture technology, including See & Spray and autonomous tractors, is sold as a technology subscription layered on top of the hardware, which is changing the revenue mix toward recurring software.

Investment case

  • Precision agriculture subscriptions add recurring, high-margin revenue to a cyclical equipment business.
  • Dealer network and brand loyalty among farmers are extraordinarily durable.
  • Global food demand grows steadily while arable land does not, which supports long-term equipment intensity.

Key risks

  • Farm incomes have fallen with lower crop prices, and equipment purchases follow farm income closely.
  • High interest rates make equipment financing expensive for buyers.
  • Used equipment inventories have built up, which delays new purchases.
