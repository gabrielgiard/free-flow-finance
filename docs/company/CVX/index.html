About Chevron Corporation

Another Standard Oil descendant, founded in 1879 as the Pacific Coast Oil Company. Mike Wirth is CEO; headquarters moved from California to Houston in 2024. Chevron's core assets are the Permian Basin, the Tengiz field in Kazakhstan following a large expansion project, deepwater Gulf of Mexico, and Australian liquefied natural gas at Gorgon and Wheatstone. The 2025 acquisition of Hess added a stake in the same Guyana development that Exxon operates. It runs a smaller refining footprint than Exxon and has historically prioritised the dividend above almost everything else.

Investment case

  • The Hess acquisition secured a share of the highest-quality oil development of the last decade.
  • Tengiz expansion adds significant low-cost production volume with the capital already spent.
  • A long dividend growth record and lower leverage than most integrated peers.

Key risks

  • Kazakhstan exposure carries political and export-route risk, particularly with regional conflict elevated.
  • Higher beta to oil prices than Exxon because of the smaller downstream buffer.
  • Venezuela and other frontier operations face sanction risk.
