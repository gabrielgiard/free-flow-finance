About Keysight Technologies, Inc.

Keysight is the original Hewlett-Packard business: the electronic test and measurement instruments Bill Hewlett and Dave Packard started with in 1939. It became part of Agilent in 1999 and was spun off as Keysight in 2014. Satish Dhanasekaran has been chief executive since 2022. Engineers use its oscilloscopes, signal analysers and design software to develop and test chips, wireless networks, AI data-centre links, cars and defence systems.

Investment case

  • Every new technology standard — faster AI links, 6G, new chips — needs new test equipment.
  • The market leader, with a growing share of sales from software and services.
  • High margins and a strong balance sheet.

Key risks

  • Orders swing with customers' research budgets.
  • Exposure to China and to export restrictions.
  • Recent acquisitions need to be integrated.
