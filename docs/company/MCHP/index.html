About Microchip Technology Incorporated

Founded in 1987 when General Instrument spun out its microelectronics division. Steve Sanghi, who ran the company from 1991 to 2021, returned as CEO in November 2024 after his successor departed amid a severe downturn. Microchip makes microcontrollers — the small, cheap processors embedded in industrial equipment, cars and appliances — alongside analog and connectivity parts. Its parts sell for a few dollars and stay designed into products for a decade or more.

Investment case

  • Extremely long product lifecycles: designs stay in production for a decade or more, making revenue durable once won.
  • Tens of thousands of customers with no meaningful concentration risk.
  • Sanghi's return brought back the operator who built the company's original discipline.

Key risks

  • Went through one of the deepest inventory corrections in the industry, with revenue nearly halving from peak.
  • Carries meaningful debt from the Microsemi acquisition into a downturn.
  • Industrial and automotive demand recovery has repeatedly been pushed out.
