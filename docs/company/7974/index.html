About Nintendo Co., Ltd.

Founded in Kyoto in 1889 to make playing cards. Shuntaro Furukawa has been president since 2018. Nintendo makes its own consoles and the games for them, starring Mario, Zelda and Pokémon (in which it holds a stake). The Switch 2, launched in June 2025, became the fastest-selling console ever. It is also building films and theme parks around its characters. Priced in yen, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • The Switch 2 is off to a record start.
  • Characters with decades of popularity, now in films and parks.
  • A huge cash pile.

Key risks

  • Console sales are cyclical; year three or four is usually weaker.
  • Memory chip costs could squeeze hardware margins.
  • Dependence on a few big game releases.
