About AbbVie Inc.

Spun out of Abbott Laboratories in 2013 to separate the research pharmaceutical business. Robert Michael became CEO in 2024. AbbVie is built around immunology: Skyrizi and Rinvoq, the two successors to Humira, which was for years the best-selling drug in the world before losing exclusivity in 2023. It also owns Botox and the Allergan aesthetics portfolio, oncology assets including Imbruvica and Venclexta, and a neuroscience franchise with Vraylar and Ubrelvy. The stock rose 11.15% in the week to 21 July 2026.

Investment case

  • Skyrizi and Rinvoq have more than replaced Humira revenue, which is the hardest transition in pharma and AbbVie executed it.
  • Aesthetics and Botox provide a cash-pay revenue stream insulated from drug pricing politics.
  • One of the highest dividend yields among large-cap pharma with a strong coverage ratio.

Key risks

  • Net debt above $60B from the Allergan acquisition is high relative to peers.
  • The immunology category is crowded and biosimilar pressure eventually reaches Skyrizi and Rinvoq too.
  • Aesthetics demand is discretionary and softened when consumers tightened spending.
