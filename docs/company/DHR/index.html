About Danaher Corporation

Founded in 1969 as a real estate trust and rebuilt as an industrial conglomerate by the Rales brothers. Rainer Blair has been CEO since 2020. Danaher has spent the last decade transforming itself into a pure life sciences and diagnostics company, spinning off its industrial businesses as Fortive and Veralto. The Danaher Business System — a rigorous continuous-improvement methodology applied to every acquisition — is the operational core of the company and is widely imitated.

Investment case

  • Cytiva and Pall give Danaher a leading position in bioprocessing, the equipment used to manufacture biologic drugs.
  • Around 75% of revenue is recurring consumables and services.
  • The Danaher Business System has a long, verifiable record of improving acquired businesses.

Key risks

  • Bioprocessing demand fell sharply as customers worked through pandemic-era inventory, and the recovery has been slow.
  • China is a meaningful market and remains weak.
  • Valuation still reflects a premium quality rating that recent growth has not supported.
