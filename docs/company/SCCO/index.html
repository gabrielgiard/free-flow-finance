About Southern Copper Corporation

Incorporated in 1952 to develop copper deposits in southern Peru, and about 89% owned by Grupo Mexico, the mining group controlled by the Larrea family. Southern Copper runs large open-pit mines, smelters and refineries in Peru and Mexico and holds the largest copper reserves of any listed company. It also produces molybdenum, silver and zinc. Its costs are among the lowest in the industry, and it has a long list of new projects in Peru awaiting development.

Investment case

  • Enormous, long-lived reserves at very low cost.
  • Copper demand is rising with electrification and data centres while new mines are scarce.
  • Pays out most of its profit as dividends.

Key risks

  • Profits follow the copper price.
  • New mines in Peru have been delayed for years by local opposition.
  • A controlling shareholder, and few shares available to trade.
