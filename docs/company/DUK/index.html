About Duke Energy Corporation

Traces to 1904 and the Catawba Power Company. Harry Sideris became CEO in 2025, succeeding Lynn Good. Duke Energy is one of the largest US regulated utilities, serving around 8.4 million electricity customers across the Carolinas, Florida, Indiana, Ohio and Kentucky. As a regulated monopoly it earns an authorised return on its invested capital base, which makes earnings unusually predictable — and makes capital investment, rather than sales growth, the main driver of profit.

Investment case

  • Regulated monopoly with authorised returns produces highly predictable earnings.
  • Data centre load growth in the Southeast is driving the strongest demand outlook in decades.
  • Large capital expenditure programme grows the rate base, and with it earnings.

Key risks

  • Very heavy debt load, and higher interest rates raise financing costs directly.
  • Returns are set by state regulators, so political decisions cap the upside.
  • Hurricane and storm exposure in the Carolinas and Florida creates recurring restoration costs.
