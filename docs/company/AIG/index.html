About American International Group, Inc.

Founded in Shanghai in 1919 by Cornelius Vander Starr. Peter Zaffino, CEO from 2021, spent his tenure simplifying a company that required a government rescue in 2008, divesting life and retirement (the last Corebridge shares were sold in May 2026) and refocusing on general insurance. Eric Andersen succeeded him as CEO in 2026, and Zaffino left the board in September 2026. AIG now writes commercial property and casualty and specialty lines globally.

Investment case

  • Underwriting discipline has improved substantially and consistently under Zaffino.
  • The simplification into a pure general insurer makes the business far easier to value.
  • A large buyback programme funded by divestiture proceeds.

Key risks

  • The 2008 legacy still colours how the market perceives risk management here.
  • Catastrophe exposure is inherent to commercial property insurance.
  • Commercial pricing has passed its cyclical peak in several lines.
