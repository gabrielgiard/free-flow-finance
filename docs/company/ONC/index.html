About BeOne Medicines Ltd.

Founded in Beijing in 2010 as BeiGene by John Oyler and scientist Xiaodong Wang; renamed BeOne Medicines and moved its legal home to Switzerland in 2025. Oyler is CEO. Its main product, Brukinsa, is a leading treatment for blood cancers such as chronic lymphocytic leukaemia, and it has a large cancer pipeline run largely from China at low cost. It turned profitable in 2025.

Investment case

  • Brukinsa keeps taking share in a large market.
  • Clinical trials run in China cost far less than in the West.
  • Profitable now, with margins rising quickly.

Key risks

  • Depends heavily on one drug.
  • US-China tensions could hit a company built across both.
  • Competition from newer blood-cancer treatments.
