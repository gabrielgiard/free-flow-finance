About Adobe Inc.

Founded in 1982 by John Warnock and Charles Geschke, who left Xerox PARC to commercialise PostScript. Shantanu Narayen has been CEO since 2007 and executed the industry's most successful subscription transition. Adobe sells Creative Cloud (Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere, After Effects), Document Cloud (Acrobat and PDF, a format Adobe invented) and Experience Cloud for enterprise marketing analytics. Its Firefly generative models are trained on licensed content and embedded directly into the creative tools.

Investment case

  • Photoshop and Acrobat are verbs. Professional workflows and file formats are extremely hard to displace.
  • Firefly's licensed training data is a real commercial advantage for enterprises worried about copyright exposure.
  • Consistently converts over a third of revenue into free cash flow.

Key risks

  • Generative AI lowers the barrier to creating images and video, which threatens the value of the tools themselves.
  • Growth has slowed to high single digits and the market has de-rated the stock accordingly.
  • New AI-native design competitors are winning the low end, where Adobe's pricing is least defensible.
