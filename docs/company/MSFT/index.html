About Microsoft Corporation

Founded in 1975 by Bill Gates and Paul Allen in Albuquerque, now headquartered in Redmond, Washington. Satya Nadella has been CEO since 2014 and rebuilt the company around cloud. Three segments: Intelligent Cloud (Azure, the number-two public cloud, plus SQL Server and GitHub), Productivity and Business Processes (Microsoft 365, Teams, Dynamics, LinkedIn) and More Personal Computing (Windows, Xbox, Surface, Bing). Its partnership with OpenAI put Copilot into Office, Windows and GitHub, and Azure is the primary cloud for a large share of commercial AI workloads.

Investment case

  • Microsoft 365 sits inside essentially every large enterprise, which makes it the lowest-friction distribution channel for AI features on earth.
  • Azure is compounding in the high teens off an enormous base while gross margins keep improving.
  • Genuinely diversified — no single product failure breaks the story, which is rare at this market cap.

Key risks

  • Capex has exploded to fund AI data centres, and the return on that spend is still unproven.
  • Copilot seat adoption has been slower to convert into revenue than the initial enthusiasm implied.
  • The OpenAI relationship has become more complex and less exclusive as OpenAI diversifies its compute suppliers.

What the street says

The most widely held mega-cap, and once again treated as the 'safe' AI exposure: the shares rose about 39% in the July-September 2026 quarter, including a 4% gain on 25 September after a Copilot overhaul that added coding tools and a persistent AI agent. Melius Research upgraded the stock to buy on 5 October 2026, arguing that AI-safety worries favour Microsoft as a trusted enterprise vendor. The live debate remains whether AI capex is building a durable moat or simply compressing free cash flow.
