About American Electric Power Company, Inc.

Traces to 1906. Bill Fehrman has been chief executive since 2024. AEP is one of the largest US electric utilities, serving about 5.6 million customers across 11 states from Ohio to Texas, and owns the largest electricity transmission network in the country. Data centre demand in Ohio, Texas and other states has led it to plan one of the largest capital spending programmes among US utilities.

Investment case

  • Data centre demand drives strong, regulated rate base growth.
  • Owns the largest US transmission network, which needs major expansion.
  • Regulated returns make earnings predictable.

Key risks

  • Huge capital spending needs new equity and debt.
  • Regulators may resist the rate increases needed.
  • Data centre demand forecasts could prove too optimistic.
