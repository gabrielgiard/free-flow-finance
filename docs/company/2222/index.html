About Saudi Arabian Oil Company (Saudi Aramco)

Created in 1933 as a concession to Standard Oil of California and fully nationalised by 1980; the Saudi government still owns over 80% directly and through its wealth fund. Amin Nasser has been CEO since 2015. Aramco is the world's largest oil producer, pumping about one in eight barrels globally at the lowest cost in the industry. Priced in Saudi riyals, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • The lowest production costs and largest reserves in the world.
  • Pays one of the largest dividends of any company.
  • Spare capacity gives flexibility.

Key risks

  • Output is set by OPEC+ policy, not by shareholders.
  • The government relies on its dividend, which limits investment flexibility.
  • Long-term oil demand risk.
