About The Goldman Sachs Group

Founded in 1869 by Marcus Goldman, a German immigrant trading commercial paper in New York. David Solomon has been CEO since 2018. Goldman is the pre-eminent investment bank: mergers and acquisitions advisory, equity and debt underwriting, and one of the largest trading operations in the world. It also runs Asset and Wealth Management, which is the strategic priority because fee revenue is more stable than trading revenue. Goldman was lead-left bookrunner on the SpaceX IPO in June 2026, the largest offering in history.

Investment case

  • Dominant market share in advisory means Goldman captures a disproportionate share of any capital markets recovery.
  • The SpaceX IPO mandate demonstrates its position at the top of the league tables, and a wave of large listings is expected to follow.
  • Asset management fee growth reduces earnings volatility over time.

Key risks

  • Earnings are inherently cyclical and depend on deal activity that can stop abruptly.
  • Higher rates and geopolitical stress can freeze the IPO and M&A pipeline the bull case depends on.
  • The consumer banking strategy was an expensive failure and management credibility took damage from it.
