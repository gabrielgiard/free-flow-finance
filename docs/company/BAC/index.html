About Bank of America Corporation

Traces to the Bank of Italy, founded in San Francisco in 1904 by Amadeo Giannini to lend to immigrants other banks ignored. Now headquartered in Charlotte, North Carolina, with Brian Moynihan as CEO since 2010. It is the second-largest US bank by assets, with the largest retail deposit base in the country. Segments: Consumer Banking, Global Wealth and Investment Management (which includes Merrill), Global Banking, and Global Markets. Its deposit franchise is unusually low-cost, which makes it highly sensitive to the level of interest rates.

Investment case

  • The cheapest large deposit base in US banking, which is the single most valuable asset a bank can own.
  • Merrill and the private bank generate stable fee revenue that is not rate-dependent.
  • Higher-for-longer rates, now the base case after the Fed's 2026 shift, are directly accretive to net interest income.

Key risks

  • A large held-to-maturity securities portfolio carries substantial unrealised losses that only unwind with time.
  • Consumer credit deteriorates quickly if unemployment rises, and the 2026 oil shock pressures household budgets.
  • Less diversified than JPMorgan in trading and investment banking.
