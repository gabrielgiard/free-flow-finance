About General Dynamics Corporation

Formed in 1952, with roots in the Electric Boat Company founded in 1899. Phebe Novakovic has been CEO since 2013. General Dynamics builds nuclear submarines through Electric Boat, combat vehicles including the Abrams tank, and IT systems for government — and separately owns Gulfstream, the business jet manufacturer. That combination of defence and luxury aviation is unique among the primes and gives it a quite different profile from Lockheed or Northrop.

Investment case

  • Submarine construction is a sole-source capability with a decades-long backlog under the Columbia and Virginia programmes.
  • AUKUS and the broader naval build-out support demand well beyond the current decade.
  • Gulfstream provides commercial diversification the other defence primes lack.

Key risks

  • Submarine production has faced well-documented supply chain and workforce constraints causing delays.
  • Business jet demand is cyclical and tied to corporate profits and high-net-worth wealth.
  • Fixed-price defence contracts expose the company to cost inflation it cannot pass on.
