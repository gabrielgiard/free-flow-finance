About The Travelers Companies, Inc.

Traces to 1864, when it was founded in Hartford, Connecticut, to insure travellers. Alan Schnitzer has been chief executive since 2015. Travelers is one of the largest US property and casualty insurers, focused on commercial insurance for businesses of every size, plus personal auto and home insurance. It is known for disciplined underwriting and heavy investment in data and claims technology.

Investment case

  • Disciplined underwriting produces consistent profits through the cycle.
  • Higher interest rates lift investment income on its large bond portfolio.
  • Long record of buybacks and dividend growth.

Key risks

  • Catastrophe losses from storms and wildfires are rising.
  • Commercial insurance pricing is softening after several strong years.
  • Lawsuit costs (social inflation) push up liability claims.
