About L3Harris Technologies, Inc.

Formed in 2019 by the merger of L3 Technologies and Harris Corporation, whose roots go back to 1895. Sam Mehta, who joined in 2023 and previously ran its space and communications businesses, became CEO in August 2026 after Christopher Kubasik stepped down following a board investigation into his conduct. L3Harris makes tactical radios, electronic warfare systems, night vision equipment and space sensors, and acquired Aerojet Rocketdyne in 2023, adding solid rocket motors — a capability with only two suppliers in the United States and one that missile production depends on entirely.

Investment case

  • Aerojet Rocketdyne is one of only two US solid rocket motor suppliers, and in September 2026 Lockheed Martin awarded seven-year propulsion contracts worth $4.7 billion for PAC-3 MSE and more than $6 billion for THAAD.
  • Tactical communications demand has risen sharply with global rearmament.
  • Positioned as a lower-cost, faster alternative to the traditional primes.

Key risks

  • Almost entirely dependent on US and allied defence budgets.
  • Expanding constrained solid rocket motor capacity requires heavy investment, including a $3 billion build-out and new plants in Arkansas, and the large PAC-3 and THAAD propulsion contracts are still undefinitized.
  • Fixed-price development contracts carry cost overrun risk.
