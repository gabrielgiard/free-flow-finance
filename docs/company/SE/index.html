About Sea Limited

Founded in 2009 by Forrest Li as Garena, a games platform in Singapore. Li is chairman and chief executive. Sea now runs three businesses: Shopee, the largest e-commerce marketplace in Southeast Asia and a major player in Brazil; Garena, whose mobile game Free Fire is one of the most played in the world; and Monee (formerly SeaMoney), a digital payments and lending business built on Shopee's customers. After years of heavy losses it turned profitable in 2024 and has kept expanding margins since.

Investment case

  • Shopee has emerged from the Southeast Asian price war as the clear market leader, with TikTok Shop the main remaining rival.
  • Monee's lending book compounds on top of Shopee's customer data at attractive margins.
  • Garena's Free Fire throws off cash that funds the rest of the group.

Key risks

  • TikTok Shop is backed by a far larger parent and can choose to spend heavily on share.
  • Lending in emerging markets carries credit risk that has not yet been tested by a deep downturn.
  • Garena depends heavily on a single ageing game.
