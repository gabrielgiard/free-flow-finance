About BHP Group Limited

Founded in 1885 as the Broken Hill Proprietary Company at a silver and lead mine in New South Wales. Mike Henry is CEO, headquartered in Melbourne. BHP is the largest mining company in the world by market value. Its dominant asset is Western Australian iron ore, among the lowest-cost operations globally, supplying Chinese steel mills. It also produces copper at Escondida in Chile and Olympic Dam in Australia, metallurgical coal for steelmaking, and is developing the Jansen potash project in Canada. Copper is the strategic growth priority.

Investment case

  • Copper demand is structurally supported by electrification, grid investment and data centre construction.
  • Western Australian iron ore has among the lowest costs per tonne in the industry, so it stays profitable at low prices.
  • Jansen potash adds a genuinely new commodity exposure with long-life reserves.

Key risks

  • Iron ore earnings depend almost entirely on Chinese construction, which has been structurally weak.
  • Mining is capital intensive with long lead times and frequent cost overruns.
  • Commodity price volatility makes earnings and dividends unpredictable.
