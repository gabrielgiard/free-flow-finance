About S&P Global Inc.

Traces to Henry Varnum Poor's 1860 publication analysing railroad finances. Martina Cheung became CEO in November 2024. S&P Global operates one of the two dominant credit rating agencies, owns the S&P 500 and other benchmark indices, and sells market data and commodity price assessments through Platts. The 2022 IHS Markit merger substantially expanded the data business. The index franchise is the crown jewel: passive funds pay a fee based on assets tracking indices S&P owns.

Investment case

  • The index business collects fees on trillions of passive assets with almost no incremental cost.
  • Credit ratings is a regulated duopoly with Moody's, and issuers have no practical alternative.
  • Recurring subscription revenue across data businesses gives high visibility.

Key risks

  • Ratings revenue is tied to debt issuance volumes, which fall sharply when rates rise.
  • Regulatory attention to rating agency conflicts of interest never fully goes away.
  • Investors worry that AI tools could erode demand for parts of the data and analytics business, a concern that has weighed on the shares through 2026.
