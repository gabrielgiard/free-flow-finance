About IREN Limited

Founded in 2018 by brothers Daniel and Will Roberts, who run it as co-chief executives, and known as Iris Energy until 2024. IREN builds and owns large data centres in Texas and British Columbia with their own grid connections. It began by mining bitcoin and is now converting those sites to house the graphics chips used for AI, which it rents out as a cloud service; a multi-year contract with Microsoft worth close to $10 billion signalled the change.

Investment case

  • Owns land and power connections that take years to obtain.
  • A long contract with one of the world's most creditworthy customers.
  • Much more power capacity still to develop.

Key risks

  • Buying chips and building sites requires billions in borrowed money.
  • The chips lose value quickly, and AI rental prices may fall.
  • Bitcoin mining profits are volatile.
