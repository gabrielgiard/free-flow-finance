About CSX Corporation

Formed in 1980 from the merger of Chessie System and Seaboard Coast Line, with predecessor railroads dating to the 1820s. Steve Angel, previously CEO of Linde, took over in 2025 after board dissatisfaction with operating performance. CSX operates around 20,000 route miles across the eastern United States, hauling intermodal containers, chemicals, agricultural products and coal between Atlantic ports and inland markets.

Investment case

  • Rail networks are irreplaceable infrastructure with essentially no prospect of new entrants.
  • Eastern network serves dense population centres and major Atlantic ports.
  • New leadership with a strong operational record brought in specifically to improve service metrics.

Key risks

  • Service and operating performance have lagged western peers, which prompted the leadership change.
  • Coal remains a meaningful revenue source and is in structural decline.
  • Industrial volumes are cyclical and have been soft.
