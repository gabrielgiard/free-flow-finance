About adidas AG

Founded in 1949 by Adi Dassler in the same small town where his brother founded Puma. Bjørn Gulden has been CEO since 2023 and led a fast turnaround after the end of the Yeezy partnership. adidas is the world's second-largest sportswear brand, strongest in football, and its Samba, Gazelle and Spezial trainers became fashion hits. Priced in euros from Frankfurt, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • Market share gains from Nike.
  • Margins have room to rise further.
  • The 2026 World Cup is a large marketing moment.

Key risks

  • The terrace-trainer trend could fade.
  • US tariffs on products made in Asia.
  • Currency swings hurt reported results.
