About Aptiv PLC

Aptiv traces back to Delphi, spun out of General Motors in 1999, and took its current name in 2017 when it split off its powertrain business. Kevin Clark has been chief executive since 2015. Aptiv designs the electronic architecture of cars: the software, computing platforms, sensors and high-speed connectors that make driver assistance and connected features work. In April 2026 it completed the spin-off of its wiring harness business as a separate company, Versigent, leaving Aptiv as a smaller, higher-margin technology supplier.

Investment case

  • Content per vehicle rises as cars carry more electronics and software.
  • The spin-off left a higher-margin, faster-growing business.
  • Its connectors are also used in industrial and data-centre markets beyond cars.

Key risks

  • Global car production is flat to falling and very sensitive to the economy.
  • Automakers are taking more software development in-house.
  • Tariffs and supply chain costs squeeze supplier margins.
