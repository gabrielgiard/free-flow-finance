About Fair Isaac Corporation

Founded in 1956 by Bill Fair and Earl Isaac, a mathematician and an engineer. Will Lansing has been chief executive since 2012. FICO created the credit score that most US lenders use to approve mortgages, cards and auto loans, and it earns a fee every time a score is pulled. It also sells decision-making software to banks. Over the past decade it has raised score prices sharply, making it one of the most profitable companies in the market.

Investment case

  • The FICO score is embedded in the US credit system, giving it exceptional pricing power.
  • Very high margins and heavy share buybacks.
  • Mortgage score revenue rises sharply when home lending recovers.

Key risks

  • Housing regulators have allowed lenders to use the rival VantageScore for mortgages.
  • Political scrutiny of its price increases.
  • Its 2025 direct licensing programme for mortgage scores changes relations with the credit bureaus.
