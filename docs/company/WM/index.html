About Waste Management, Inc.

Founded in 1968 by Dean Buntrock and Wayne Huizenga, who consolidated hundreds of small local haulers. James Fish has been CEO since 2016. Waste Management is the largest waste collection and disposal company in North America, and its most valuable assets are landfills — permitting a new one is close to impossible in most jurisdictions, which makes existing capacity extraordinarily durable.

Investment case

  • Landfill permits are effectively irreplaceable — this is one of the strongest asset moats in industrials.
  • Waste volumes are stable and largely recession-resistant.
  • Renewable natural gas from landfill methane is a growing high-margin revenue stream.

Key risks

  • Recycling commodity prices are volatile and can swing segment profitability.
  • Fuel and labour costs are significant and inflation-sensitive.
  • The Stericycle acquisition added medical waste, a new business with integration risk.
