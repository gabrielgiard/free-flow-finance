About Monster Beverage Corporation

Traces to Hansen's, a 1935 juice company, transformed after Rodney Sacks and Hilton Schlosberg acquired it in 1992 and launched Monster Energy in 2002. Schlosberg is now CEO. Monster is the second largest energy drink brand globally behind Red Bull, and its distribution agreement with Coca-Cola gives it access to a global network it could never have built alone. Energy drinks remain one of the fastest-growing beverage categories.

Investment case

  • The Coca-Cola distribution partnership provides global reach with no capital investment.
  • Energy drinks continue to take share from carbonated soft drinks, particularly among younger consumers.
  • Very high margins and a net cash balance sheet.

Key risks

  • Celsius and other entrants have taken meaningful share in the US.
  • Aluminium and ingredient cost inflation compresses gross margin.
  • Regulatory attention to caffeine content in drinks marketed to young people is a recurring risk.
