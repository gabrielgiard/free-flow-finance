About Intuitive Surgical

Founded in 1995 to commercialise robotic surgery research from SRI International. Gary Guthart has been CEO since 2010. Intuitive makes the da Vinci surgical system — a robotic platform that lets a surgeon operate through small incisions using instruments controlled from a console — and Ion, a robotic catheter for lung biopsy. The business model is razor-and-blade: systems are placed with hospitals, then Intuitive earns recurring revenue on instruments and accessories consumed in every procedure, plus service contracts.

Investment case

  • Recurring instrument revenue grows with procedure volume, independent of new system placements.
  • Surgeons train on da Vinci during residency, which creates a professional lock-in that competitors cannot buy their way past.
  • Enormous installed base of systems already placed, each generating consumable revenue for a decade.

Key risks

  • The valuation is very demanding for a medical device company, on any earnings measure.
  • Medtronic, J&J and several Chinese manufacturers are launching credible competing platforms.
  • Hospital capital budgets tighten when credit conditions do, and system placements are the first thing deferred.
