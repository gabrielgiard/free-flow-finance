About AST SpaceMobile, Inc.

Founded in 2017 by Abel Avellan, who is chief executive. AST SpaceMobile is building a network of very large satellites that connect directly to ordinary mobile phones, providing voice and broadband in places with no masts. Rather than selling to consumers, it works through mobile operators — AT&T, Verizon, Vodafone and others — who would offer the service to their existing customers and share the revenue. It has launched its first commercial satellites and needs dozens more for continuous coverage.

Investment case

  • Agreements with operators that together serve billions of subscribers.
  • Its satellites have much larger antennas than rivals', allowing real broadband speeds.
  • Government and defence interest provides early revenue.

Key risks

  • Revenue is tiny today, and the full network needs many more successful launches.
  • SpaceX's Starlink is pursuing the same market with far greater resources.
  • Has funded itself by repeatedly issuing shares and convertible debt.
