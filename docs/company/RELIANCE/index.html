About Reliance Industries Limited

Founded in 1966 by Dhirubhai Ambani as a textile trading business. His son Mukesh Ambani has led it since 2002 and turned it into India's largest company. Three engines: the original oil-to-chemicals business, including the world's largest single-site refinery at Jamnagar; Jio, which built a nationwide 4G and 5G network from scratch and became India's largest mobile operator with over 520 million customers; and Reliance Retail, the country's largest retailer by revenue. Jio Platforms is preparing an initial public offering in October 2026, and Retail is also expected to list separately. Prices shown per share in US dollar terms.

Investment case

  • Jio has enormous operating leverage — the network is built and every additional subscriber is close to pure margin.
  • Indian consumption growth is one of the strongest structural stories in global equities.
  • The Jio Platforms IPO, expected to open on 21 October 2026 as India's largest at about $3.8 billion, should crystallise value the conglomerate structure obscures.

Key risks

  • The oil-to-chemicals business is capital intensive and exposed to refining margin volatility.
  • Capital allocation is controlled by a single family, which limits minority shareholder influence.
  • Telecom tariff increases in India are politically sensitive and hard to push through.
