About Ford Motor Company

Founded in 1903 by Henry Ford, who introduced the moving assembly line and changed manufacturing permanently. James Farley has been CEO since 2020 and reorganised the company into three units: Ford Blue for combustion vehicles, Model e for electric, and Ford Pro for commercial customers. Ford Pro is the standout — commercial fleet customers buy trucks, then buy service, parts and software subscriptions for years afterwards.

Investment case

  • Ford Pro generates high-margin recurring service and software revenue from commercial fleets.
  • The F-Series has been the best-selling US vehicle line for more than four decades.
  • Net debt figures look alarming but largely reflect Ford Credit's lending book, not industrial leverage.

Key risks

  • The electric vehicle division has been losing billions annually with no near-term path to profitability.
  • Warranty and recall costs have been persistently higher than peers.
  • Tariffs and supply chain disruption directly affect a company assembling vehicles across borders.
