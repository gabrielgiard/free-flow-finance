About NextEra Energy, Inc.

Founded in 1925 as Florida Power & Light. John Ketchum is CEO, headquartered in Juno Beach, Florida. NextEra is two businesses in one: FPL, a regulated utility serving about 12 million Floridians with predictable, rate-regulated returns; and NextEra Energy Resources, the largest generator of wind and solar power in the world, which sells electricity under long-term contracts. Data centre power demand has transformed the outlook for the competitive business, and NextEra has restarted work on nuclear capacity to serve it.

Investment case

  • AI data centres need enormous quantities of firm, clean power and are willing to sign twenty-year contracts to get it.
  • The regulated Florida utility provides stable, growing earnings in a state with strong population growth.
  • The largest renewable development pipeline in the country, with land and interconnection queue positions already secured.

Key risks

  • Utilities are heavily leveraged and rising interest rates directly raise the cost of that capital.
  • US federal policy support for renewables has become less certain.
  • Florida is exposed to hurricane damage and the associated restoration costs.
