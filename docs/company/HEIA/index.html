About Heineken N.V.

Founded in Amsterdam in 1864 by Gerard Adriaan Heineken and still controlled by the family through Heineken Holding. The world's second-largest brewer, with Heineken, Amstel, Tiger and Birra Moretti and strong positions in Europe, Latin America and Africa. Dolf van den Brink stepped down as CEO in May 2026 and the board named Rafael Oliveira, formerly head of JDE Peet's, as his successor. Priced in euros from Amsterdam, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • Premium Heineken brand keeps growing faster than beer overall.
  • Emerging markets give long-term volume growth.
  • Cost savings programme.

Key risks

  • Beer volumes are falling in many markets as people drink less.
  • Currency losses in Africa and Latin America.
  • A new CEO from outside the beer industry.
