About Warner Bros. Discovery, Inc.

Created in 2022 when Discovery merged with WarnerMedia, spun out of AT&T. David Zaslav has been CEO since the merger. The company owns Warner Bros. studios, HBO, CNN, Discovery's cable networks and the HBO Max streaming service. It has announced a separation of its declining cable networks from its studios and streaming business — an acknowledgement that the two have fundamentally different prospects.

Investment case

  • HBO has one of the strongest content brands in television and a genuine prestige reputation.
  • The Warner Bros. film and television library is a deep and durable asset.
  • Separating declining networks from growing streaming should clarify valuation for both.

Key risks

  • Enormous debt taken on at the merger, in a business with declining cash flow.
  • Linear television advertising and affiliate fees are in steep structural decline.
  • Streaming reached profitability only recently and at modest levels relative to the scale required.
