About Occidental Petroleum Corporation

Founded in 1920 and built into a major by Armand Hammer. Richard Jackson was promoted to president and chief executive in 2026, succeeding Vicki Hollub, who had led the company since 2016 as the first woman to run a large US oil producer. Occidental produces oil and gas primarily in the Permian Basin, the Gulf of Mexico and the Middle East, and sold its OxyChem chemicals business to Berkshire Hathaway in a $9.7 billion deal completed at the start of 2026. Berkshire Hathaway holds a large stake. The company is also the most committed major to direct air capture, building carbon removal facilities in Texas.

Investment case

  • High-quality Permian acreage with low breakeven costs.
  • Free cash flow before working capital reached $3.0 billion in the second quarter of 2026, the highest since the third quarter of 2022.
  • Berkshire's continued accumulation is a meaningful signal of long-term confidence.

Key risks

  • Leverage built up through the 2019 Anadarko acquisition and the 2024 CrownRock deal still matters, although principal debt fell to $11.8 billion in the second quarter of 2026.
  • Direct air capture is capital-intensive and its economics remain unproven at scale.
  • Preferred shares held by Berkshire carry an 8% dividend that consumes cash flow.
