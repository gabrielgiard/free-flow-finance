About Blackstone Inc.

Founded in 1985 by Stephen Schwarzman and Peter Peterson with $400,000. Schwarzman still runs it. Blackstone is the largest alternative asset manager in the world, investing in private equity, real estate, credit and infrastructure on behalf of pension funds, sovereign wealth funds and, increasingly, individual investors. It earns management fees on committed capital plus performance fees on gains, and its real estate business is the largest of any private owner.

Investment case

  • Management fees on long-locked capital produce highly predictable base revenue.
  • The private wealth channel is a genuinely large new source of capital that has barely been tapped.
  • Scale allows participation in transactions no competitor can finance alone.

Key risks

  • Performance fees are volatile and depend on realising investments at good prices.
  • Commercial real estate, historically its strongest area, has faced severe valuation pressure.
  • Higher interest rates raise financing costs across every leveraged strategy.
