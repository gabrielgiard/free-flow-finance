About Comfort Systems USA, Inc.

Formed in 1997 by combining a group of local contractors. Brian Lane has been chief executive since 2011. Comfort Systems installs and services heating, cooling, plumbing and electrical systems in large buildings through dozens of local subsidiaries. Data centres and chip factories, which need enormous cooling and power systems, now account for a large share of its work, and it builds much of the equipment in its own factories as modules that are shipped to site.

Investment case

  • A record order backlog, led by data centres.
  • Skilled labour is scarce, which gives established contractors pricing power.
  • Factory-built modules raise productivity and margins.

Key risks

  • Growth leans heavily on continued data-centre construction.
  • Fixed-price contracts carry execution risk.
  • The share price has multiplied and assumes the boom continues.
