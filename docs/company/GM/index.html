About General Motors Company

Founded in 1908 by William Durant in Flint, Michigan; reorganised through bankruptcy in 2009. Mary Barra has been CEO since 2014, the first woman to lead a major global automaker. GM sells Chevrolet, GMC, Buick and Cadillac vehicles, with full-size pickup trucks and SUVs generating the overwhelming majority of profit. Its Ultium battery platform underpins its electric vehicles, GM Financial provides lending, and it operates Cruise autonomous driving after significantly scaling back that programme. Aggressive buybacks have materially reduced the share count.

Investment case

  • Full-size trucks and SUVs remain enormously profitable and face limited competitive pressure.
  • The share count has fallen sharply through buybacks, which lifts earnings per share independent of growth.
  • Trades at a very low multiple of earnings, with the market assigning almost no value to future growth.

Key risks

  • Electric vehicle investment has consumed capital without yet generating returns.
  • US tariffs raise input costs on parts sourced from Mexico and Canada.
  • The Chinese joint ventures, once a profit source, are now barely breaking even.
