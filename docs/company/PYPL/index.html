About PayPal Holdings, Inc.

Founded in 1998 as Confinity, merged with Elon Musk's X.com, acquired by eBay in 2002 and spun back out in 2015. Alex Chriss, previously of Intuit, became CEO in late 2023. PayPal processes online payments for merchants and consumers, owns Venmo and Braintree, and handles well over a trillion dollars of payment volume annually. The strategic problem is that its branded checkout business is highly profitable while its unbranded processing business is not.

Investment case

  • Around 430 million active accounts create genuine two-sided network effects.
  • Venmo is culturally embedded with younger US consumers and remains substantially under-monetised.
  • Strong free cash flow and an aggressive buyback at a depressed multiple.

Key risks

  • Transaction margin has compressed as low-margin Braintree volume grew faster than branded checkout.
  • Apple Pay, Shop Pay and other native checkout options attack the core branded business directly.
  • A long record of guidance revisions has damaged management credibility.
