About Workday, Inc.

Founded in 2005 by Dave Duffield and Aneel Bhusri after Oracle's hostile takeover of their previous company, PeopleSoft. Carl Eschenbach became CEO in 2023. Workday sells cloud human resources and financial management software to large enterprises. Replacing an HR system of record is one of the most disruptive projects a company can undertake, which makes both winning and losing customers a slow process — and revenue extremely sticky once won.

Investment case

  • Systems of record for HR and finance have exceptionally high switching costs.
  • Gross retention above 95% reflects how rarely customers leave.
  • Large enterprise customer base with long contract durations and high visibility.

Key risks

  • Headcount-based pricing means slower corporate hiring directly slows revenue growth.
  • SAP and Oracle compete hard, particularly in finance where Workday is less established.
  • Growth has decelerated from the rates that originally justified the multiple.
