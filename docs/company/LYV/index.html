About Live Nation Entertainment, Inc.

Formed in 2010 by the merger of Live Nation, the concert promoter spun out of Clear Channel in 2005, with Ticketmaster. Michael Rapino has been chief executive since 2005. Live Nation is the largest live entertainment company in the world: it promotes concerts, owns or operates hundreds of venues, manages artists, and runs Ticketmaster, the dominant ticketing platform. Sponsorship and advertising at its events earn the highest margins.

Investment case

  • Spending on live events has kept growing as fans prioritise experiences.
  • Ticketmaster and sponsorship are high-margin businesses tied to the concert flywheel.
  • Owning venues lets it capture food, drink and parking revenue.

Key risks

  • The US Justice Department sued in 2024 seeking to break up the company, which could force it to sell Ticketmaster.
  • Concerts themselves earn thin margins.
  • Ticket fees attract political and consumer anger.
