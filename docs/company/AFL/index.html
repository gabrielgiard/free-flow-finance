About Aflac Incorporated

Founded in 1955 by brothers John, Paul and Bill Amos. Dan Amos has been chief executive since 1990, one of the longest tenures at any large US company. Aflac sells supplemental insurance that pays cash directly to policyholders when they fall ill or are injured, to cover costs their main insurance does not. Although famous in America for its duck advertisements, it earns most of its profit in Japan, where it insures roughly one in four households against cancer.

Investment case

  • Very profitable, with policies people keep for decades.
  • Has raised its dividend every year for more than four decades.
  • Buys back a large amount of stock each year.

Key risks

  • Sales in Japan have been shrinking as the population ages.
  • A weak yen reduces the dollar value of Japanese earnings.
  • Growth is slow in both markets.
