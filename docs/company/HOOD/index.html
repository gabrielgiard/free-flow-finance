About Robinhood Markets, Inc.

Founded in 2013 by Vladimir Tenev and Baiju Bhatt, who introduced commission-free stock trading and forced the entire brokerage industry to follow. Tenev still runs it. Robinhood earns revenue from payment for order flow, net interest on customer cash and margin lending, crypto trading, and a subscription product called Gold. Its customer base skews young, which is strategically valuable as those customers accumulate assets over time.

Investment case

  • Dominant brand among younger investors, a demographic traditional brokers have struggled to reach.
  • Diversifying into retirement accounts, credit cards and wealth management deepens the relationship.
  • Crypto trading provides revenue that traditional brokerages largely cannot access.

Key risks

  • Revenue is highly sensitive to retail trading activity, which collapses in bear markets.
  • Payment for order flow remains a target for regulatory prohibition, particularly in Europe.
  • Crypto revenue is volatile and dependent on speculative cycles.
