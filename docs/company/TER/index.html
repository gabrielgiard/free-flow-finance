About Teradyne, Inc.

Founded in 1960 by two MIT graduates, Alex d'Arbeloff and Nick DeWolf. Greg Smith has been CEO since 2020. Teradyne makes automated test equipment — the systems that verify a chip works correctly before it ships. Every semiconductor is tested, so Teradyne sells into the entire industry regardless of who wins. It also owns Universal Robots and Mobile Industrial Robots, a collaborative robotics business that has grown more slowly than hoped.

Investment case

  • Test intensity rises with chip complexity, so AI and advanced packaging structurally increase test time per device.
  • A duopoly with Advantest in the highest-end test systems.
  • Net cash balance sheet and consistent buybacks.

Key risks

  • Highly cyclical, following semiconductor capital spending closely.
  • Customer concentration is significant among the largest chipmakers.
  • The robotics business has consistently disappointed against its original acquisition thesis.
