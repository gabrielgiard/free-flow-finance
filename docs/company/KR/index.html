About The Kroger Co.

Founded in 1883 by Barney Kroger, who invested his life savings of $372 in a Cincinnati grocery store. Ronald Sargent, previously chairman, became CEO in 2025. Kroger is one of the largest US grocers, operating around 2,700 stores under Kroger, Ralphs, Fred Meyer and other regional banners. Its proposed merger with Albertsons was blocked by courts in 2024, forcing a return to organic strategy.

Investment case

  • Grocery is genuinely recession-resistant — people eat regardless of the economy.
  • Own-brand products carry higher margins and have gained share as consumers seek value.
  • Retail media, selling advertising to suppliers, is a small but very high-margin growth area.

Key risks

  • Grocery operates on razor-thin margins, around 2%, leaving little room for error.
  • Walmart, Costco and Aldi all compete aggressively on price.
  • The blocked Albertsons merger removed the scale strategy management had been pursuing.
