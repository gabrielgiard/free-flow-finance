About Tempus AI, Inc.

Founded in 2015 by Eric Lefkofsky, the co-founder of Groupon, after his wife was treated for cancer. He is chief executive. Tempus sequences the DNA of patients' tumours to help doctors choose treatments, and has assembled one of the largest libraries of clinical and genetic data, which it licenses to drug companies for research. It listed in 2024 and bought the hereditary-testing company Ambry Genetics in 2025.

Investment case

  • Each test adds to a data library that becomes more valuable as it grows.
  • Most large drug makers already buy its data.
  • Testing volumes are rising quickly and losses are narrowing.

Key risks

  • Not yet consistently profitable.
  • Reimbursement for genetic tests is uncertain.
  • Competes with Guardant, Natera, Foundation Medicine and Caris.
