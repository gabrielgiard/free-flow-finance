About Newmont Corporation

Founded in 1921 by William Boyce Thompson. Natascha Viljoen became chief executive in January 2026, succeeding Tom Palmer. Newmont is the world's largest gold miner, with mines in North and South America, Australia, Africa and Papua New Guinea, and it also produces copper. It bought Australia's Newcrest Mining in 2023 and has since sold non-core mines to focus on its best assets. With gold above $4,000 an ounce, it generates record free cash flow.

Investment case

  • Record gold prices drive record free cash flow.
  • A focused portfolio of long-life mines after divestments.
  • Large buybacks and debt reduction.

Key risks

  • Earnings move almost entirely with the gold price.
  • Mining costs and project delays have hurt it before.
  • Operations in countries with political risk.
