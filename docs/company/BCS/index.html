About Barclays PLC

Traces its roots to a goldsmith banking business in the City of London in 1690. C. S. 'Venkat' Venkatakrishnan has led it since 2021. Barclays combines a large UK retail and business bank, Barclaycard, a US consumer card business and a global investment bank that is one of the few European rivals to Wall Street in markets and dealmaking. A 2024 plan aims to shift capital toward the steadier UK businesses and lift returns. One US-listed share represents four London shares.

Investment case

  • Trading and dealmaking revenue are strong as markets stay busy.
  • The plan targets a return on tangible equity above 12%, which the market has not fully priced.
  • Large buybacks: billions of pounds are returned every year.

Key risks

  • The investment bank makes earnings volatile and uses a lot of capital.
  • UK politics and possible bank taxes can hit domestic profits.
  • US card losses would rise quickly in an American recession.
