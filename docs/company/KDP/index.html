About Keurig Dr Pepper Inc.

Formed in 2018 when the coffee-pod company Keurig merged with Dr Pepper Snapple, whose flagship drink dates from 1885. Tim Cofer has been chief executive since 2024. It sells Dr Pepper, 7UP, Canada Dry, Snapple and a growing energy-drink range in the US, and Keurig brewers and pods. In August 2025 it agreed to buy the Dutch coffee group JDE Peet's for about 16 billion euros, with a plan to then split into two listed companies: one for soft drinks and one for coffee.

Investment case

  • Dr Pepper has gained market share for years and is now the number two US soda.
  • Its own delivery network makes it a partner of choice for new drink brands.
  • Splitting into two focused companies could attract a higher valuation for soft drinks.

Key risks

  • The JDE Peet's deal adds a great deal of debt, and investors disliked it.
  • Single-serve coffee has been shrinking in the US, and coffee bean prices are volatile.
  • Executing a large acquisition and a break-up together is complicated.
