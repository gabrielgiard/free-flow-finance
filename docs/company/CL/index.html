About Colgate-Palmolive Company

Founded in 1806 by William Colgate as a soap and candle business in New York. Noel Wallace has been CEO since 2019. Colgate sells toothpaste, toothbrushes, soap and home care products, and owns Hill's Pet Nutrition, a premium veterinary-recommended pet food business that has become the growth engine. Colgate toothpaste holds roughly 40% global market share, an extraordinary position for a consumer category.

Investment case

  • Around 40% global toothpaste share, with particular strength in emerging markets.
  • Hill's Pet Nutrition benefits from the same pet humanisation trend as Zoetis, with better growth than the core business.
  • Oral care is a daily habit purchase, about as recession-resistant as consumer spending gets.

Key risks

  • Heavy emerging market exposure creates significant currency translation risk.
  • Private label competition in developed markets pressures volumes.
  • Growth has come more from price than volume, which is not indefinitely sustainable.
