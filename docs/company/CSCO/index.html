About Cisco Systems, Inc.

Founded in 1984 by Leonard Bosack and Sandy Lerner, two Stanford staff who wanted to connect incompatible campus networks. Chuck Robbins has been CEO since 2015. Cisco makes the routers, switches and security products that carry most enterprise network traffic, and has been shifting from selling hardware boxes to selling software subscriptions. The 2024 Splunk acquisition added a large observability and security analytics business.

Investment case

  • Enormous installed base and switching costs — replacing enterprise networking gear is disruptive and rarely worth it.
  • AI data centres need vastly more networking capacity, which plays to Cisco's core.
  • The subscription shift makes revenue more predictable and supports a higher multiple.

Key risks

  • Arista and white-box vendors have taken meaningful share in high-performance data-centre switching.
  • Growth is low single digit; this is a share-buyback-and-dividend story more than a growth one.
  • Splunk was expensive and integration risk is real.
