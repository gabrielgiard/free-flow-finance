About The Hershey Company

Founded in 1894 by Milton Hershey, who built a town around his chocolate factory and left control of the company to a trust that funds a school for children in need; the trust still holds most of the votes. Kirk Tanner, formerly of PepsiCo and Wendy's, became chief executive in August 2025. Hershey is the largest chocolate maker in the United States, with Reese's, Hershey's and Kit Kat, and has added salty snacks such as SkinnyPop and Dot's Pretzels.

Investment case

  • Reese's and Hershey's dominate American chocolate aisles.
  • Cocoa prices have fallen from their record highs, which should restore margins.
  • Salty snacks give it a second growth business.

Key risks

  • Record cocoa costs squeezed profits and forced large price rises.
  • Shoppers are buying less confectionery at higher prices, and weight-loss drugs may reduce snacking.
  • Almost entirely dependent on the US.
