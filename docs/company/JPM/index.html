About JPMorgan Chase & Co.

Traces its roots to the Bank of the Manhattan Company, chartered in 1799, and consolidates JP Morgan, Chase Manhattan, Bank One and Bear Stearns. Jamie Dimon has been CEO since 2005, one of the longest tenures in global banking. It is the largest US bank, with roughly $4.8T in assets under management at the end of 2025. Four segments: Consumer and Community Banking, Corporate and Investment Bank (trading, advisory, underwriting), Commercial Banking, and Asset and Wealth Management. It partnered with Coinbase in July 2026 to expand into digital assets.

Investment case

  • A fortress balance sheet that lets it gain share in every crisis — it acquired distressed banks in 2008 and again in 2023.
  • Scale advantage in technology spending is enormous; it can outspend every regional bank combined.
  • Diversified revenue means trading strength offsets lending weakness and vice versa.

Key risks

  • Credit costs rise if the 2026 oil shock and higher rates push the consumer into stress.
  • Net interest income benefits fade if the Fed cuts, and the market is currently pricing hikes instead.
  • Dimon's eventual succession is a genuine, if long-signalled, risk.
