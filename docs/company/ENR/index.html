About Siemens Energy AG

Spun off from Siemens in 2020. Christian Bruch has been CEO since then and steered it through a 2023 crisis at its wind turbine unit, Siemens Gamesa, which required a German government guarantee. The other businesses boomed: gas turbines and power grid equipment are in short supply worldwide as data centres and electrification drive demand. The shares rose more than tenfold from the 2023 low. Priced in euros from Frankfurt, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • Gas turbine backlog sold out for years as AI data centres need power.
  • Grid equipment demand is booming.
  • Wind losses are shrinking.

Key risks

  • Siemens Gamesa onshore wind could still lose money.
  • A high valuation after a huge run.
  • Cyclical power equipment markets.
