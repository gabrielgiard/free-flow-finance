About The TJX Companies, Inc.

Founded in 1976 with the first T.J. Maxx store. Ernie Herrman has been CEO since 2016. TJX operates T.J. Maxx, Marshalls and HomeGoods, buying excess inventory from brands and department stores at steep discounts and selling it at prices well below full retail. The treasure-hunt format — constantly changing assortment, no guarantee an item will be there next visit — drives unusually high visit frequency and has proved remarkably resistant to e-commerce.

Investment case

  • The off-price model gains share in downturns as consumers trade down, and holds it in recoveries.
  • Buying opportunistically means the model actually benefits from industry-wide inventory gluts.
  • Treasure-hunt merchandising is genuinely difficult to replicate online.

Key risks

  • Depends on other retailers over-ordering; a disciplined industry reduces available inventory.
  • Very limited e-commerce presence compared with the rest of retail.
  • Freight and wage inflation compress margins in a low-gross-margin model.
