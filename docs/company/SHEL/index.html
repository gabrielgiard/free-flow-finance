About Shell plc

Formed in 1907 by the merger of Royal Dutch Petroleum and the Shell Transport and Trading Company. Wael Sawan became CEO in 2023 and moved the sole headquarters to London. Shell is the largest liquefied natural gas trader in the world, which is its genuine differentiator — it buys, ships and sells LNG globally and earns trading margin on the volatility. It also has upstream oil production, a large refining and chemicals arm, and one of the biggest branded fuel retail networks. Sawan has scaled back renewable investment to prioritise cash returns.

Investment case

  • The LNG trading business earns money from volatility itself, which is a rare and genuinely differentiated capability.
  • European gas security concerns have structurally raised the value of flexible LNG supply.
  • Trades at a persistent discount to US integrated majors despite comparable cash generation.

Key risks

  • European energy policy and windfall taxes create political risk US peers do not face.
  • The retreat from renewables has attracted criticism from European institutional investors.
  • The valuation discount to Exxon and Chevron has persisted for years and shows no sign of closing.
