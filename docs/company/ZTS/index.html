About Zoetis Inc.

Spun out of Pfizer in 2013, with roots in Pfizer's animal health division dating to 1952. Kristin Peck has been CEO since 2020. Zoetis is the largest animal health company in the world, making vaccines, parasiticides and medicines for both pets and livestock. The companion animal business is the attractive part: pet owners increasingly treat animals as family members and spend accordingly, and veterinary medicines face no insurance reimbursement pressure.

Investment case

  • Pet humanisation is a durable spending trend largely insulated from economic cycles.
  • No payer or reimbursement pressure, unlike human pharmaceuticals — owners pay directly.
  • Librela and Solensia, monoclonal antibodies for osteoarthritis pain, opened a genuinely new category.

Key risks

  • Pet adoption normalised after the pandemic surge, slowing new patient formation.
  • Livestock demand is tied to protein prices and farm economics.
  • Reports of adverse events with Librela have created some prescriber caution.
