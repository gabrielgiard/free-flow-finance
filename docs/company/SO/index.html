About The Southern Company

Founded in 1945. Christopher Womack has been CEO since 2023. Southern Company serves around 9 million customers across Georgia, Alabama and Mississippi through its regulated utilities. It completed Vogtle Units 3 and 4 in 2023 and 2024 — the first newly constructed nuclear reactors in the United States in over three decades, delivered years late and billions over budget, but now producing carbon-free baseload power exactly as data centre demand accelerates in Georgia.

Investment case

  • Vogtle gives Southern the largest nuclear generating capacity of any US utility, valuable for carbon-free baseload.
  • Georgia is one of the fastest-growing data centre markets in the country.
  • Constructive regulatory relationships in its core states.

Key risks

  • Vogtle's cost overruns were severe and the recovery has been contentious with regulators.
  • High leverage in a higher-rate environment.
  • Nuclear operations carry low-probability but high-severity risk.
