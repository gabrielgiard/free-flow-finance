About Royal Bank of Canada

Founded in 1864 in Halifax, Nova Scotia, as the Merchants Bank of Halifax. Dave McKay has been CEO since 2014, headquartered in Toronto. RBC is Canada's largest bank and one of the most profitable in the world, operating in an oligopoly where five banks hold the overwhelming majority of domestic deposits. Segments span Personal Banking, Commercial Banking, Wealth Management including City National in the US, Capital Markets and Insurance. Its 2024 acquisition of HSBC Canada consolidated the domestic market further.

Investment case

  • Canadian banking is a protected oligopoly with structurally higher returns on equity than US retail banking.
  • The HSBC Canada acquisition removed a competitor and added deposits at attractive terms.
  • Long unbroken dividend record supported by conservative underwriting.

Key risks

  • Canadian household debt relative to income is among the highest in the developed world.
  • Housing market exposure is concentrated and mortgage renewals at higher rates pressure borrowers.
  • Growth is limited by the size of the domestic market.
