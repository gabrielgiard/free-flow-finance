About Rivian Automotive, Inc.

Founded in 2009 by RJ Scaringe, who still runs it. Rivian makes electric pickup trucks and SUVs, plus delivery vans for Amazon, which is also a major shareholder. Its R2 platform, a smaller and substantially cheaper vehicle, is the company's attempt to reach a mainstream market — the current R1 vehicles are premium-priced and volumes are far too low to cover fixed costs. Volkswagen has invested in a joint venture for Rivian's software and electrical architecture.

Investment case

  • Genuinely well-regarded products with strong owner satisfaction scores.
  • The Volkswagen joint venture validates Rivian's software architecture and provides substantial funding.
  • R2 targets a much larger addressable market at a far lower price point.

Key risks

  • Still loses money on every vehicle at current volumes, and the cash burn is substantial.
  • R2 execution is existential — the company needs it to work and to be on time.
  • Competing against Tesla, legacy manufacturers and Chinese entrants simultaneously.
