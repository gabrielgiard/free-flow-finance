About Boston Scientific Corporation

Founded in 1979 by John Abele and Peter Nicholas to commercialise less-invasive medical devices. Michael Mahoney has been CEO since 2012 and has overseen one of the strongest turnarounds in the sector. Boston Scientific makes cardiovascular devices, endoscopy equipment and urology products. Its Farapulse pulsed field ablation system, used to treat atrial fibrillation, has been the fastest device launch in the company's history.

Investment case

  • Farapulse is winning pulsed field ablation share rapidly in a large and growing electrophysiology market.
  • Consistently the fastest organic growth among large medical device companies.
  • Well-executed acquisition strategy that has repeatedly bought growth at reasonable prices.

Key risks

  • Medtronic and Johnson & Johnson are both pushing hard into pulsed field ablation.
  • Growth expectations embedded in the valuation are high.
  • Integration risk from a steady stream of bolt-on acquisitions.
