About Sandisk Corporation

Founded in 1988 by Eli Harari, Sanjay Mehrotra and Jack Yuan, who helped invent the flash memory card. Western Digital bought the company in 2016 and spun it off again in February 2025; David Goeckeler, who ran Western Digital, became its chief executive. Sandisk makes NAND flash memory — the chips that store data in phones, laptops and data-centre drives — in joint-venture factories with Kioxia in Japan. A shortage of memory driven by AI data-centre demand sent prices and profits up very sharply through 2026.

Investment case

  • AI servers need far more flash storage, and supply has been tight.
  • Shares the cost of factories with Kioxia, which lowers the capital needed.
  • Net cash on the balance sheet after a very profitable year.

Key risks

  • Memory is the most cyclical corner of semiconductors and today's margins are unlikely to last.
  • Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron and Kioxia can all add supply.
  • The share price has moved enormously in both directions.
