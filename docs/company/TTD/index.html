About The Trade Desk, Inc.

Founded in 2009 by Jeff Green, who still runs it. The Trade Desk operates a demand-side platform: software that advertising agencies and brands use to buy digital advertising across the open internet — connected television, audio, display — everywhere except the walled gardens of Google, Meta and Amazon. Its independence from any media owner is the core of the pitch.

Investment case

  • Independent positioning means no conflict of interest, unlike platforms that also sell their own inventory.
  • Connected television advertising is shifting from linear TV budgets and is growing quickly.
  • UID2 is a credible industry alternative to third-party cookies.

Key risks

  • Amazon has entered demand-side advertising aggressively with enormous scale advantages.
  • Advertising spend is cyclical and among the first budgets cut.
  • A high multiple that leaves no room for the growth deceleration seen recently.
