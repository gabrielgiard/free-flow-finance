About Paychex, Inc.

Founded in 1971 by Tom Golisano with $3,000 and a credit card. John Gibson has been chief executive since 2022. Paychex runs payroll, HR, benefits and insurance services for more than 800,000 small and mid-sized US businesses. It bought Paycor, a mid-market payroll software company, for about $4.1 billion in 2025. It also earns interest on client payroll funds it holds between collection and payment.

Investment case

  • Payroll is a sticky, essential service with high retention.
  • Paycor adds larger clients and cross-selling opportunities.
  • Strong margins and a high dividend payout.

Key risks

  • Growth depends on small business formation and hiring.
  • Lower interest rates reduce income on client funds.
  • Cloud-native rivals compete for new small businesses.
