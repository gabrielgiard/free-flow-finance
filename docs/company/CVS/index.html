About CVS Health Corporation

Founded in 1963 as Consumer Value Stores. David Joyner became CEO in October 2024 after a period of severe underperformance. CVS is unusually vertically integrated: it owns retail pharmacies, Caremark (a large pharmacy benefit manager), and Aetna (a major health insurer). The theory is that owning the whole chain lowers costs; the practice has been that problems in any one part drag down the whole.

Investment case

  • Vertical integration across pharmacy, benefits management and insurance is genuinely difficult to replicate.
  • Around 9,000 retail locations put a pharmacy within reach of most of the US population.
  • Valuation is depressed enough that modest operational improvement could re-rate the shares.

Key risks

  • Medicare Advantage utilisation ran far above expectations, causing repeated guidance cuts.
  • Retail pharmacy faces structural pressure from mail order and Amazon.
  • Very heavy debt load following the Aetna acquisition constrains flexibility.
