About Rio Tinto Group

Founded in 1873 when investors bought ancient copper mines along Spain's Rio Tinto river. Dual-listed in London and Sydney; Simon Trott became CEO in 2025. Rio Tinto is the world's second-largest mining company. Its Pilbara iron ore operations in Western Australia are the profit engine. It also produces aluminium, where it is one of the largest Western producers, copper at Kennecott, Escondida and the enormous Oyu Tolgoi underground mine in Mongolia, and lithium following the Arcadium acquisition. Oyu Tolgoi is the key growth asset.

Investment case

  • Oyu Tolgoi ramps toward becoming one of the largest copper mines in the world through the 2030s.
  • Aluminium exposure benefits from electrification and Western supply constraints.
  • The Arcadium acquisition gave real lithium scale at a cyclical low in lithium prices.

Key risks

  • Same Chinese iron ore dependence as BHP, and in August and September 2026 China's state buyer CMRG told some steel mills to pause purchases of Rio's Pilbara Blend to strengthen its negotiating position.
  • Mongolian political risk affects the single most important growth project.
  • Simandou in Guinea will add substantial new iron ore supply to an already well-supplied market.
