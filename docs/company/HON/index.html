About Honeywell International

Traces to a 1906 thermostat business and the 1999 merger of AlliedSignal and Honeywell. Vimal Kapur is CEO. Honeywell is a diversified industrial announcing a break-up into separate aerospace, automation and advanced materials companies. Aerospace makes avionics, auxiliary power units and engines. Building Automation supplies control systems and fire safety. Industrial Automation covers process control and warehouse robotics. Advanced Materials produces specialty chemicals and refrigerants. It also owns Quantinuum, one of the leading quantum computing companies.

Investment case

  • The planned separation should remove the conglomerate discount and let each business be valued on its own merits.
  • Aerospace aftermarket is performing strongly alongside the wider recovery in flight hours.
  • Quantinuum is a genuinely valuable quantum asset that carries almost no weight in the current valuation.

Key risks

  • Break-ups are complex, expensive and take years to complete.
  • Industrial automation demand is tied to capital spending cycles that have been soft.
  • Growth has been mediocre for several years and the separation is partly an admission of that.
