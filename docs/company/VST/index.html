About Vistra Corp.

Emerged in 2016 from the bankruptcy of Energy Future Holdings, the former TXU. Jim Burke has been chief executive since 2022. Vistra is one of the largest US power generators, with gas, coal and nuclear plants, mainly in Texas and the eastern PJM market, plus TXU Energy, a large electricity retailer. Its 2024 acquisition of Energy Harbor made it the second-largest owner of US nuclear plants, and it has signed power supply deals for data centres.

Investment case

  • Rising power demand from data centres and electrification lifts prices.
  • Nuclear plants are valuable for data centre deals.
  • Strong free cash flow funds large buybacks.

Key risks

  • Earnings are exposed to wholesale power prices and weather.
  • Coal plants face environmental costs and closures.
  • Political and regulatory changes in Texas and PJM.
