About Joby Aviation, Inc.

Founded in 2009 by JoeBen Bevirt, who is chief executive. Joby is developing a battery-powered aircraft that takes off and lands like a helicopter and flies like a plane, carrying a pilot and four passengers on short city trips with far less noise. It is working through the final stages of US certification and plans to start services in Dubai first. Toyota is its largest outside shareholder and manufacturing partner, and it bought Blade's helicopter passenger business in 2025 to gain routes and customers.

Investment case

  • Furthest ahead among air-taxi developers in US certification.
  • Backing and factory expertise from Toyota, and partnerships with Delta and Uber.
  • More than two billion dollars of cash to fund the launch.

Key risks

  • Spends several hundred million dollars a year with very little revenue.
  • Certification, and then public acceptance, could take longer than planned.
  • Whether air taxis can be run at a profit is unproven.
