About Unilever PLC

Formed in 1929 by the merger of the Dutch Margarine Unie and the British soap maker Lever Brothers. Fernando Fernandez, previously finance chief, became chief executive in March 2025. Unilever sells Dove, Vaseline, Rexona, Hellmann's, Knorr, Liquid I.V. and hundreds of other brands in about 190 countries, with more than half its sales in emerging markets. It demerged its ice cream business as The Magnum Ice Cream Company in December 2025.

Investment case

  • Strong brands with leading positions in emerging markets such as India and Indonesia.
  • The ice cream demerger leaves a faster-growing, higher-margin portfolio.
  • Focus on 30 power brands is improving growth.

Key risks

  • Consumer goods volumes are weak in developed markets.
  • Emerging-market currencies hurt reported results.
  • Activist and investor pressure has forced repeated strategy changes.
