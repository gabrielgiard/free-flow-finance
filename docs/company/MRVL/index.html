About Marvell Technology, Inc.

Founded in 1995 by Sehat Sutardja, Weili Dai and Pantas Sutardja. Matt Murphy became CEO in 2016 and refocused the company entirely on data infrastructure. Marvell designs custom silicon for hyperscalers — chips built to one customer's specification rather than sold off the shelf — plus the electro-optics and networking parts that move data between servers. Its custom AI silicon programmes with a small number of very large cloud customers are the whole growth story.

Investment case

  • Custom AI silicon is the one credible alternative to buying merchant GPUs, and Marvell is one of only two companies doing it at scale.
  • Electro-optics content grows with every increase in data-centre interconnect speed.
  • Design wins lock in multi-year revenue because a custom chip cannot be second-sourced.

Key risks

  • Extreme customer concentration — a handful of hyperscalers drive the majority of growth, and any one of them insourcing would be severe.
  • Broadcom is the larger and better-established custom silicon competitor.
  • The legacy consumer, carrier and enterprise businesses are shrinking and mask the underlying data-centre growth.
