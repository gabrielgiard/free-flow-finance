About Lumentum Holdings Inc.

Spun out of JDS Uniphase in 2015. Michael Hurlston became chief executive in February 2025. Lumentum makes the lasers and optical chips that turn electrical signals into light — the core component inside the optical transceivers that link servers in AI data centres — along with optical modules and switches for cloud and telecom networks. Its indium phosphide laser chips have been in short supply as data-centre demand surged.

Investment case

  • One of very few suppliers of high-speed laser chips, with demand ahead of supply.
  • New products such as optical circuit switches and co-packaged optics lasers add growth.
  • Margins are rising quickly as volumes grow.

Key risks

  • Revenue depends on a small number of very large cloud customers.
  • Optical components have a long history of boom and bust.
  • Carries convertible debt and trades on a very high multiple.
