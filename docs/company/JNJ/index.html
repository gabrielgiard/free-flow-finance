About Johnson & Johnson

Founded in 1886 by three Johnson brothers in New Brunswick, New Jersey, originally making sterile surgical dressings. Joaquin Duato is CEO. After spinning off its consumer health business as Kenvue in 2023, J&J is a focused pharmaceutical and medical technology company. Innovative Medicine covers oncology (Darzalex, Carvykti), immunology (Tremfya, Stelara) and neuroscience. MedTech sells surgical robotics through Ottava, orthopaedics, cardiovascular devices including the Abiomed heart pump, and vision care. It carries a AAA-equivalent credit rating, rarer than a triple-A sovereign.

Investment case

  • Balance sheet strength is exceptional, giving it the ability to acquire growth when others cannot afford to.
  • Diversification across pharma and devices smooths the patent cliffs that hit pure pharmaceutical companies.
  • Over six decades of consecutive dividend increases, a record almost nothing in the market matches.

Key risks

  • Stelara has lost exclusivity and biosimilar erosion is a real revenue headwind.
  • Talc litigation remains unresolved and the potential liability is difficult to size with confidence.
  • Mid single-digit growth means the stock is a yield and stability holding, not a compounder.
