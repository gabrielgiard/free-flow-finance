About Commonwealth Bank of Australia

Founded by the Australian government in 1911 and privatised in the 1990s; today Australia's largest bank and at times the most valuable bank in the world relative to its earnings. Matt Comyn has been CEO since 2018. CBA has about a quarter of Australia's home loans and household deposits and the country's most-used banking app. Priced in Australian dollars, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • The strongest retail franchise in Australia, with technology rivals cannot match.
  • Returns on equity around 13-14% with very low credit losses.
  • A favourite holding of Australian retirement funds, which supports demand for the shares.

Key risks

  • Trades at more than three times book value, far above global banks with similar returns.
  • Growth is tied to Australian housing and mortgages.
  • Many analysts rate it a sell purely on valuation.
