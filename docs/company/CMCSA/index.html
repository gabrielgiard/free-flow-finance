About Comcast Corporation

Founded in 1963 by Ralph Roberts, whose son Brian Roberts has been CEO since 2002. Comcast is the largest US cable broadband provider and owns NBCUniversal — the broadcast network, Universal film studio, theme parks and the Peacock streaming service. The Epic Universe park opened in Orlando in 2025. Its central problem is that broadband, the profit engine, has stopped growing as fixed wireless and fibre competitors take share.

Investment case

  • Broadband remains a high-margin, essential service with substantial infrastructure barriers.
  • Theme parks are irreplaceable assets and Epic Universe is a major new capacity addition.
  • The content library and studio have real value in a consolidating media landscape.

Key risks

  • Broadband subscriber losses to fixed wireless and fibre have become persistent rather than temporary.
  • Peacock loses money and lacks the scale of Netflix or Disney+.
  • Traditional cable television subscriptions continue to decline every quarter.
