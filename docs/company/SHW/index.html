About The Sherwin-Williams Company

Founded in 1866 by Henry Sherwin and Edward Williams in Cleveland. Heidi Petz became CEO in 2024. Sherwin-Williams makes and sells paint and coatings, and its structural advantage is distribution: it operates around 5,000 company-owned stores serving professional painters, who value availability and colour consistency far more than price. That direct relationship with contractors is something competitors selling through hardware retailers cannot replicate.

Investment case

  • Company-owned store network creates a direct, sticky relationship with professional painters.
  • Paint is a small share of a repainting job's total cost, so contractors are relatively price-insensitive.
  • Repaint demand is more stable than new construction, since surfaces need recoating regardless.

Key risks

  • New residential construction and housing turnover both drive demand and are rate-sensitive.
  • Raw material costs, particularly petrochemical-derived inputs, are volatile.
  • Consumer DIY paint demand normalised sharply after the pandemic surge.
