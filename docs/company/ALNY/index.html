About Alnylam Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

Founded in 2002 to turn RNA interference, a Nobel Prize-winning discovery, into medicines. Yvonne Greenstreet has been chief executive since 2022. RNAi drugs silence the gene that produces a harmful protein, often with injections only a few times a year. Alnylam's largest product, Amvuttra, was approved in 2025 to treat ATTR amyloidosis with cardiomyopathy, a large heart disease market, which has driven very fast sales growth and pushed the company into profitability.

Investment case

  • Amvuttra's cardiomyopathy launch is one of the strongest in biotech.
  • A platform that has produced several approved drugs, with more in the pipeline, including for hypertension.
  • Turning profitable, with margins set to rise quickly.

Key risks

  • Competition in ATTR cardiomyopathy from Pfizer's tafamidis and BridgeBio's acoramidis.
  • Heavy reliance on one drug family.
  • Drug pricing reform could affect high-priced rare disease medicines.
