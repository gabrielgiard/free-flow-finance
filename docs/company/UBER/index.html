About Uber Technologies

Founded in 2009 by Garrett Camp and Travis Kalanick. Dara Khosrowshahi has been CEO since 2017 and turned a cash-burning growth story into a free-cash-flow generating platform. Uber operates ride-hailing in roughly 70 countries, Uber Eats delivery, and Uber Freight logistics. It also sells advertising against its app surface, a small but very high-margin revenue line. Rather than build its own autonomous vehicles, Uber has partnered with Waymo and other AV developers to put their vehicles on its demand network.

Investment case

  • Two-sided network effects in each city — more drivers means shorter waits, which brings more riders.
  • The AV partnership strategy converts the biggest existential threat into a supply source Uber does not have to fund.
  • Advertising revenue is high margin and still small relative to the app's reach.

Key risks

  • Driver classification law remains unresolved in several major markets and could add substantial cost.
  • If autonomous fleets scale, the vehicle owners may capture the economics and relegate Uber to a booking layer.
  • Delivery is a competitive, low-margin business with well-funded rivals in every region.
