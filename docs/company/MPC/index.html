About Marathon Petroleum Corporation

Spun out of Marathon Oil in 2011, with refining roots dating to 1887. Maryann Mannen became CEO in 2024. Marathon Petroleum is the largest US refiner by capacity, running thirteen refineries, and holds a controlling stake in MPLX, a midstream partnership that provides steadier fee-based cash flow. It also operates the Speedway-branded fuel supply relationship following that chain's sale.

Investment case

  • Largest US refining capacity, with scale advantages in crude sourcing and product distribution.
  • MPLX distributions provide predictable cash flow largely independent of refining margins.
  • Among the most aggressive capital return programmes in the sector.

Key risks

  • Refining margins are extremely volatile and outside management control.
  • Long-term fuel demand faces pressure from vehicle electrification.
  • Refinery turnarounds and unplanned outages can significantly affect quarterly results.
