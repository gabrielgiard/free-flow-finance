About Aon plc

Formed in 1982 through the combination of Ryan Insurance and Combined International. Greg Case has been CEO since 2005. Aon is the second largest insurance broker globally, placing commercial risk and advising on reinsurance, plus a substantial human capital consulting business. Like Marsh McLennan it earns commission on premium without underwriting any risk itself. In August 2026 it agreed to buy USI, a US middle-market broker with about $3 billion of annual revenue, for $17.0 billion in cash, with completion expected in the fourth quarter of 2026.

Investment case

  • Broking captures the upside of rising insurance prices without bearing any claims.
  • Reinsurance broking is a concentrated market with very few credible participants.
  • USI adds a large US middle-market platform, with about $395 million of targeted annual run-rate synergies on top of highly recurring renewal revenue.

Key risks

  • After NFP, the debt-funded $17 billion USI purchase takes leverage to about 4.8 times at closing, against a 2.8–3.0 times target Aon aims to regain within about 24 months.
  • Commission revenue falls when insurance pricing softens.
  • Consulting revenue is discretionary and cyclical, and buybacks are paused while debt is repaid.
