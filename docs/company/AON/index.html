About Aon plc

Formed in 1982 through the combination of Ryan Insurance and Combined International. Greg Case has been CEO since 2005. Aon is the second largest insurance broker globally, placing commercial risk and advising on reinsurance, plus a substantial human capital consulting business. Like Marsh McLennan it earns commission on premium without underwriting any risk itself.

Investment case

  • Broking captures the upside of rising insurance prices without bearing any claims.
  • Reinsurance broking is a concentrated market with very few credible participants.
  • Highly recurring revenue as programmes renew annually.

Key risks

  • Debt increased substantially with the NFP acquisition.
  • Commission revenue falls when insurance pricing softens.
  • Consulting revenue is discretionary and cyclical.
