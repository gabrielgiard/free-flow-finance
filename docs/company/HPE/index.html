About Hewlett Packard Enterprise Company

Created in 2015 when the original Hewlett-Packard, founded in 1939, split in two: HP Inc. kept PCs and printers, and Hewlett Packard Enterprise took servers, storage and networking for businesses. Antonio Neri has been chief executive since 2018. HPE closed its roughly $14 billion purchase of Juniper Networks in July 2025, which doubled the size of its networking business. It also builds AI servers and supercomputers through its Cray unit.

Investment case

  • Juniper adds a higher-margin networking business with AI data-centre exposure.
  • A large installed base of enterprise customers buying AI systems.
  • Cost savings from the Juniper integration.

Key risks

  • Servers are a low-margin, competitive business against Dell and Supermicro.
  • Integrating Juniper while competing with Cisco and Arista is demanding.
  • Debt rose to pay for the deal.
