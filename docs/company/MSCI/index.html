About MSCI Inc.

Traces to 1969, when Capital International began publishing global stock indexes; it became MSCI under Morgan Stanley and listed in 2007. Henry Fernandez has been chief executive since 1998. MSCI's indexes are the benchmarks for trillions of dollars of investments, and it earns fees from exchange-traded funds and investors licensing them. It also sells portfolio analytics, climate and sustainability data, and private markets data from Burgiss.

Investment case

  • Index benchmarks are deeply embedded and very hard to replace.
  • Fees on ETFs grow automatically as markets rise and money flows into passive funds.
  • Margins above 50% and strong buybacks.

Key risks

  • Fee revenue falls when markets fall.
  • Demand for sustainability and climate data has slowed in the US.
  • Low-cost index providers pressure pricing.
