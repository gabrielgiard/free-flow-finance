About The Walt Disney Company

Founded in 1923 by Walt and Roy Disney. Bob Iger returned as CEO in late 2022 after his chosen successor was removed. Disney owns the most valuable collection of intellectual property in entertainment — Disney animation, Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars and National Geographic — and monetises it through films, streaming, television, consumer products and theme parks. The parks and experiences division is by far the largest profit contributor.

Investment case

  • An intellectual property portfolio with no real equivalent anywhere in media.
  • Theme parks are extremely profitable and effectively impossible to replicate.
  • Streaming has reached profitability after years of heavy losses.

Key risks

  • Linear television networks are in structural decline and still represent meaningful profit.
  • ESPN's transition to direct-to-consumer risks accelerating cable subscriber losses.
  • Succession planning remains unresolved after the previous attempt failed publicly.
