About AT&T Inc.

Traces to the American Telephone and Telegraph Company incorporated in 1885. John Stankey has been CEO since 2020. After an expensive and unsuccessful diversification into media — DirecTV and Time Warner, both since divested — AT&T has refocused entirely on connectivity: wireless service for around 118 million subscribers and a fibre broadband build targeting 30 million-plus locations. The strategy is now deliberately unglamorous and the market has responded better to that than to the media era.

Investment case

  • Fibre broadband has better economics and lower churn than the copper and cable it replaces.
  • Wireless is a rational three-player market with disciplined pricing.
  • Free cash flow comfortably covers a dividend yielding well above the market.

Key risks

  • An extremely large debt load built up during the media acquisition era.
  • Wireless subscriber growth is essentially a share-shift game in a saturated market.
  • Fibre construction is capital-intensive with long payback periods.
