About Itau Unibanco Holding S.A.

Created in 2008 by merging Banco Itau with Unibanco, founded in 1924; the Setubal, Villela and Moreira Salles families still control it. Milton Maluhy Filho has been chief executive since 2021. Itau is the largest private-sector bank in Latin America, serving tens of millions of customers in Brazil with lending, cards, insurance and investment banking, plus operations in Chile, Colombia, Uruguay and Paraguay. It consistently earns a return on equity above 20%.

Investment case

  • The best-run large bank in Brazil, with returns its state-owned rivals cannot match.
  • Careful lending has kept bad loans low through several downturns.
  • Pays out a high share of profit in dividends.

Key risks

  • Brazilian interest rates, inflation and the currency are volatile.
  • Nubank and other digital banks are taking younger customers.
  • Tax changes for banks are a recurring political risk.
