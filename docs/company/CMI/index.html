About Cummins Inc.

Founded in 1919 by Clessie Cummins, a mechanic who believed in the then-unproven diesel engine. Jennifer Rumsey has been chief executive since 2022. Cummins makes diesel and natural gas engines for lorries, buses, construction and mining equipment, and the large generators that provide backup power to hospitals and data centres. Demand for data-centre generators has made power systems its most profitable business while the lorry market has been weak.

Investment case

  • Data centres are ordering backup generators years ahead.
  • The leading independent engine maker, supplying most large lorry builders.
  • A worldwide service network that earns steady parts revenue.

Key risks

  • North American lorry production is deeply cyclical.
  • Emissions rules keep changing, which shifts when customers buy.
  • Its electric and hydrogen business has lost money.
