About Kinder Morgan, Inc.

Founded in 1997 by Richard Kinder and Bill Morgan. Kim Dang has been chief executive since 2023; Richard Kinder is executive chairman. Kinder Morgan operates the largest natural gas pipeline network in the United States, moving about 40% of the country's gas, plus refined products pipelines, terminals and carbon dioxide operations. Most of its income comes from long-term fee contracts rather than commodity prices.

Investment case

  • Natural gas demand is rising from LNG exports and power for data centres.
  • Fee-based contracts make cash flow predictable.
  • A large backlog of pipeline expansion projects.

Key risks

  • High debt, although it has been reduced.
  • New pipelines face permitting and legal challenges.
  • Long-term risk if gas demand falls with the energy transition.
