About Wells Fargo & Company

Founded in 1852 by Henry Wells and William Fargo to serve the California gold rush. Charles Scharf became CEO in 2019 with a mandate to fix the bank after the 2016 fake accounts scandal, which led to an unprecedented Federal Reserve asset cap that constrained the balance sheet for seven years. Wells Fargo is one of the largest US retail banks, with a deposit franchise spanning consumer banking, commercial lending, wealth management and a growing corporate and investment bank.

Investment case

  • Removal of the Federal Reserve asset cap frees the balance sheet to grow for the first time since 2018.
  • A large, low-cost deposit base is one of the most valuable assets in banking.
  • Significant cost reduction is still available after years of elevated regulatory spending.

Key risks

  • Consumer credit quality deteriorates in a recession, and Wells has meaningful card and auto exposure.
  • Commercial real estate exposure, particularly office, remains a concern.
  • The reputational damage from the accounts scandal took years to repair and constrains regulatory goodwill.
