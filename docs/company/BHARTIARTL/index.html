About Bharti Airtel Limited

Founded in 1995 by Sunil Mittal and India's second-largest mobile operator with more than 400 million customers, plus operations in 14 African countries through Airtel Africa. Gopal Vittal led it for over a decade. With Reliance Jio, Airtel now shares most of the Indian mobile market in a near-duopoly, which has allowed repeated price increases. Priced in Indian rupees, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • Indian tariffs keep rising in a two-player market.
  • Revenue per user still very low by global standards, with room to grow.
  • Strong free cash flow as 5G spending peaks.

Key risks

  • Regulatory and government payments.
  • A premium valuation.
  • Currency swings in Africa.
