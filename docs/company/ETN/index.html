About Eaton Corporation plc

Founded in 1911 in Bloomfield, New Jersey, making truck axles. Paulo Ruiz became CEO in 2025, succeeding Craig Arnold. Eaton makes electrical power management equipment — switchgear, circuit protection, power distribution units and backup systems. It has become one of the clearest beneficiaries of data centre construction, because every megawatt of computing capacity requires substantial electrical infrastructure to deliver, distribute and protect power.

Investment case

  • Data centre electrical demand is growing faster than almost any other industrial end market.
  • Grid modernisation and electrification create a multi-year structural investment cycle.
  • Record backlog gives unusual revenue visibility for an industrial company.

Key risks

  • A meaningful share of the valuation now rests on data centre capital spending continuing.
  • Vehicle and eMobility segments are exposed to a slower-than-expected EV transition.
  • The stock has re-rated substantially, so expectations are high.
