About Copart, Inc.

Founded in 1982 by Willis Johnson with a single scrapyard in California; his son-in-law Jay Adair is executive chairman and Jeff Liaw is chief executive. When an insurer writes off a damaged car, Copart stores it and sells it through an online auction to dismantlers, repairers and exporters around the world. It owns the land its yards sit on — thousands of acres near major cities — which newcomers would struggle to assemble, and shares the market mainly with one rival.

Investment case

  • A two-company market with very high barriers to entry.
  • Cars are more expensive to repair each year, so more are written off.
  • High margins, no debt and several billion dollars of cash.

Key risks

  • Insurers have been cutting back cover, reducing the number of claims.
  • Has lost some volume to its rival.
  • Used-car prices affect auction proceeds.
