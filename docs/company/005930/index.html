About Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd.

Founded in 1969 as Samsung Electric Industries in Suwon, South Korea, part of the Samsung chaebol established in 1938. Samsung is the only company that competes at scale in memory, foundry, smartphones and displays simultaneously. Its Device Solutions division makes DRAM, NAND and logic chips; Mobile Experience makes Galaxy phones; Visual Display makes televisions; and Samsung Display supplies OLED panels including to Apple. It reported record quarterly operating profit in 2026 on the memory upcycle, and its share price still fell on the news. ADR-equivalent pricing shown.

Investment case

  • The only company with leading positions across memory, foundry, displays and handsets, which no competitor can match.
  • Record operating profit in the 2026 memory cycle demonstrates the earnings power of the semiconductor division.
  • Historically trades at a large discount to global peers because of the Korean market discount.

Key risks

  • Memory is cyclical and Samsung's earnings swing more violently than almost any other large-cap technology company.
  • Its foundry business has lost ground to TSMC and has struggled to attract external customers.
  • It has trailed SK Hynix in HBM qualification, ceding the highest-margin memory segment.
