About Honda Motor Co., Ltd.

Founded in 1948 by Soichiro Honda, starting with motorised bicycles. Toshihiro Mibe has been chief executive since 2021. Honda is the world's largest maker of motorcycles by far, a major car maker (Civic, Accord, CR-V), and also builds power equipment, marine engines and the HondaJet. Motorcycles, especially in India and Southeast Asia, earn much of its profit. Merger talks with Nissan collapsed in February 2025, and Honda has since scaled back parts of its electric vehicle plan.

Investment case

  • The world's dominant motorcycle business is highly profitable and growing in emerging markets.
  • Strong hybrid line-up in the US as buyers favour hybrids over full electric vehicles.
  • A low valuation with steady buybacks and dividends.

Key risks

  • US tariffs on Japanese and Mexican-built cars hit car profits.
  • Losing share in China to local electric vehicle makers.
  • Its electric vehicle strategy has been scaled back and lags rivals.
