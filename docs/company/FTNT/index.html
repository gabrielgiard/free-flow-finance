About Fortinet, Inc.

Founded in 2000 by Ken Xie, who had previously founded NetScreen, and his brother Michael Xie. Ken Xie still runs it. Fortinet sells network security, and its differentiator is hardware: it designs its own security processors, which let its firewalls handle inspection workloads far more efficiently than competitors running on general-purpose chips. That translates into better price-performance, particularly for mid-sized customers and distributed branch networks.

Investment case

  • Custom silicon gives a genuine cost and performance advantage that software-only competitors cannot match.
  • Very strong operating margins, among the best in cybersecurity.
  • Large installed base of firewall appliances creates a natural refresh cycle.

Key risks

  • The post-pandemic firewall refresh cycle pulled demand forward, creating a difficult comparison period.
  • More hardware-dependent than competitors, which the market rewards with a lower multiple.
  • Palo Alto, Zscaler and Microsoft all compete for the same security budgets.
