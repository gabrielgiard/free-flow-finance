About Cerebras Systems Inc.

Founded in 2016 by Andrew Feldman, who is chief executive, and colleagues from his previous server company. Cerebras makes the largest computer chip ever built: instead of cutting a silicon wafer into hundreds of processors, it uses the whole wafer as one. The design answers questions from AI models many times faster than conventional graphics chips, and Cerebras rents that speed to customers through its own data centres as well as selling systems. It listed on Nasdaq in May 2026 at $185 a share.

Investment case

  • Speed is becoming more valuable as AI models are used for real-time tasks.
  • Very large multi-year commitments from leading AI developers.
  • Cash from the listing to fund new data centres.

Key risks

  • A few customers account for most of its revenue.
  • Competes with Nvidia, the best-funded company in the industry, and with chips the cloud giants design themselves.
  • Building data centres takes enormous capital, and the company is not yet profitable.
