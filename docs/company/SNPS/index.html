About Synopsys, Inc.

Founded in 1986 by Aart de Geus, who ran it until 2024 when Sassine Ghazi took over. Synopsys makes electronic design automation software — the tools every chip company uses to design, simulate and verify silicon before it is manufactured. It also licenses silicon IP: pre-built, pre-verified circuit blocks that customers drop into their own designs. Together with Cadence it forms a duopoly that is effectively unavoidable for anyone designing a modern chip.

Investment case

  • A genuine duopoly with Cadence. Switching EDA vendors mid-design is close to impossible, so revenue is subscription-like and highly visible.
  • Every new chip design needs more verification, so tool spend rises with complexity regardless of unit volumes.
  • AI chip proliferation means more customers designing more silicon, which is exactly what EDA sells into.

Key risks

  • Growth depends on customers' R&D budgets, which are discretionary in a downturn.
  • Valuation embeds a lot of durability — this has rarely been a cheap stock.
  • China exposure is a persistent regulatory risk for design tools.
