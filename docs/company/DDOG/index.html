About Datadog, Inc.

Founded in 2010 by Olivier Pomel and Alexis Lê-Quôc, who had worked together at Wireless Generation and were frustrated by the gap between developers and operations teams. Pomel still runs it. Datadog monitors cloud infrastructure and applications, showing engineers what is happening across their systems in real time. It has expanded methodically from infrastructure monitoring into logs, security and application performance, and customers typically adopt more products over time.

Investment case

  • Land-and-expand works unusually well here — most customers add products every year.
  • Cloud migration and application complexity both structurally increase what needs monitoring.
  • Genuinely strong product engineering reputation among developers, who are the buyers.

Key risks

  • Usage-based pricing means revenue falls when customers optimise cloud spending.
  • Competes with Dynatrace, New Relic, Splunk and Grafana, plus native cloud provider tools.
  • A persistently high multiple that leaves the stock exposed to any growth deceleration.
