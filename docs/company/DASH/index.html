About DoorDash, Inc.

Founded in 2013 by Tony Xu, Andy Fang, Stanley Tang and Evan Moore, Stanford students who started delivering food from Palo Alto restaurants. Xu is chief executive. DoorDash is the largest food delivery platform in the United States and has expanded into groceries, convenience and retail delivery, plus Wolt in Europe. In 2025 it bought Deliveroo, the UK-based delivery company, and SevenRooms, a restaurant software business.

Investment case

  • Clear market leader in US food delivery with network effects in each city.
  • Groceries and retail delivery and a fast-growing advertising business extend the platform.
  • DashPass membership keeps customers ordering more often.

Key risks

  • Gig worker regulation could raise delivery costs.
  • Uber competes hard, and Amazon is a potential rival in grocery delivery.
  • Integrating Deliveroo and investing in international markets weighs on margins.
