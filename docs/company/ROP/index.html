About Roper Technologies, Inc.

Founded in 1981 as an industrial pump manufacturer. Neil Hunn has been CEO since 2018. Roper has spent two decades transforming into a collection of vertical software businesses — legal case management, freight matching, healthcare compliance, government contracting software. Each serves a niche too small for large vendors to attack but essential to the customers in it.

Investment case

  • Niche vertical software has very high retention because no credible alternative exists.
  • A disciplined acquisition machine with a long record of buying and improving businesses.
  • Around 75% recurring revenue with strong cash conversion.

Key risks

  • Growth depends on continued acquisitions, which requires reasonable valuations to be available.
  • The businesses are individually small, so any single one moves the needle little.
  • Consistently expensive relative to both software and industrial peers.
