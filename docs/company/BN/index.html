About Brookfield Corporation

Began in 1899 as a builder of tramways and power lines in Sao Paulo. Bruce Flatt has been chief executive since 2002 and is often compared to Warren Buffett for his long-term approach. Brookfield Corporation is the parent of a large family of businesses: it owns most of Brookfield Asset Management, one of the world's biggest managers of infrastructure, property, renewable power and private credit; a fast-growing insurance arm; and direct stakes in listed renewable, infrastructure and industrial companies.

Investment case

  • Owns real assets — power, pipelines, data centres — that are in heavy demand.
  • The insurance arm supplies long-term money to invest.
  • A long record of compounding value per share.

Key risks

  • A complicated structure that is hard to analyse; reported revenue includes companies it only partly owns.
  • Large borrowings at the level of its individual assets.
  • Office property remains a weak spot.
