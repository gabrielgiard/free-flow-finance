About Astera Labs, Inc.

Founded in 2017 by Jitendra Mohan, Sanjay Gajendra and Casey Morrison, three Texas Instruments engineers. Mohan is chief executive. Astera Labs makes the connectivity chips that move data between processors, memory and accelerators inside AI servers: retimers that keep signals clean over PCIe links, Scorpio switches for connecting GPUs, and memory expansion chips. It listed in March 2024 and has grown very quickly with AI server builds.

Investment case

  • Its chips sit in almost every new AI server rack, so it grows with AI spending.
  • New switch products greatly expand the market it can sell into.
  • Gross margins above 70% and strong cash generation.

Key risks

  • Dependent on a few hyperscale customers and on NVIDIA's platform choices.
  • Broadcom and Marvell compete in the same connectivity markets.
  • A very high valuation leaves little room for disappointment.
