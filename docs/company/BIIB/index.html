About Biogen Inc.

Founded in 1978, among the earliest biotechnology companies. Christopher Viehbacher became CEO in 2022. Biogen built its franchise in multiple sclerosis, now facing generic competition, and has staked its future on Alzheimer's disease — Leqembi, developed with Eisai, is one of the first treatments shown to slow cognitive decline, though uptake has been slower than hoped.

Investment case

  • Leqembi addresses an enormous unmet need with essentially no direct competition yet.
  • Deep neuroscience expertise built over four decades.
  • Trades at a low multiple with substantial cash generation from the legacy franchise.

Key risks

  • The multiple sclerosis franchise is in structural decline as generics arrive.
  • Leqembi requires diagnostic infrastructure and regular infusions, which has slowed adoption sharply.
  • A long record of pipeline disappointments has damaged credibility.
