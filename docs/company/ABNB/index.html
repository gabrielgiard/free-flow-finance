About Airbnb, Inc.

Founded in 2008 by Brian Chesky, Joe Gebbia and Nathan Blecharczyk, who started by renting air mattresses in their San Francisco apartment during a design conference. Chesky still runs it. Airbnb connects travellers with hosts offering short-term accommodation and takes a commission from both sides. The marketplace is asset-light — it owns no property — and generates exceptional free cash flow relative to revenue.

Investment case

  • Two-sided network effects with over seven million listings that no competitor can replicate quickly.
  • Asset-light model produces free cash flow margins above 35%.
  • Brand has become a generic verb, which is rare and valuable in consumer marketplaces.

Key risks

  • Regulatory restrictions on short-term rentals keep tightening in major cities.
  • Booking.com and Vrbo compete directly, and hotels have improved their direct offerings.
  • Travel demand is cyclical, and management has warned of tougher comparisons in the second half of 2026 after revenue growth reaccelerated to 17% in the second quarter.
