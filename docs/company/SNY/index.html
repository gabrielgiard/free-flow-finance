About Sanofi S.A.

Traces to 1973, taking its current form through mergers with Synthélabo and Aventis. Paul Hudson has been CEO since 2019 and refocused the company on immunology and vaccines, spinning off consumer healthcare. Dupixent, an anti-inflammatory treating eczema, asthma and several other conditions, has become one of the largest-selling drugs in the world and drives most of the growth. US-listed as an ADR.

Investment case

  • Dupixent keeps adding approved indications, each expanding the addressable population.
  • Vaccines is a structurally defensive business with high barriers to entry.
  • Trades at a discount to US pharmaceutical peers on comparable pipelines.

Key risks

  • Heavy dependence on a single product — Dupixent is a very large share of growth.
  • European pharmaceutical companies face persistent pricing pressure at home.
  • The pipeline behind Dupixent is less proven than the market would like.
