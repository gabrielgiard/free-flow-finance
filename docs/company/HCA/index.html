About HCA Healthcare, Inc.

Founded in 1968 in Nashville by Thomas Frist Sr., his son and Jack Massey, creating one of the first hospital chains. Sam Hazen has been CEO since 2019. HCA operates around 190 hospitals and 2,400 care sites, concentrated in fast-growing Sun Belt markets including Texas and Florida. Hospital economics depend heavily on payer mix — how many patients are commercially insured versus on Medicare, Medicaid, or uninsured.

Investment case

  • Scale gives real negotiating leverage with commercial insurers and suppliers.
  • Concentrated in Sun Belt markets with above-average population growth.
  • Aggressive share repurchase programme has substantially reduced the share count.

Key risks

  • Payer mix can deteriorate quickly if unemployment rises or subsidies lapse.
  • Labour costs, particularly nursing, have risen structurally since the pandemic.
  • Very high leverage, which magnifies both good and bad operating outcomes.
