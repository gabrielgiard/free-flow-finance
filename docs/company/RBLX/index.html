About Roblox Corporation

Founded in 2004 by David Baszucki and Erik Cassel. Baszucki is chief executive. Roblox is a platform where users play games built by other users and creators, who earn a share of the virtual currency, Robux, that players spend. It has well over 100 million daily users, many of them children and teenagers, and has grown rapidly in recent years on viral hits made by independent creators.

Investment case

  • Network effects: more creators attract more players, which attracts more creators.
  • Bookings and daily users have accelerated, helped by viral creator-made hits.
  • Strong free cash flow despite large investment in safety and infrastructure.

Key risks

  • Several US states have sued over child safety, and regulation could restrict younger users.
  • Engagement depends on hits it does not make itself.
  • Heavy stock-based pay dilutes shareholders.
