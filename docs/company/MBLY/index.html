About Mobileye Global Inc.

Founded in 1999 by Amnon Shashua and Ziv Aviram. Intel bought it in 2017 and floated it again in 2022, keeping a majority stake. Mobileye's EyeQ chips and software run camera-based driver assistance in a very large share of the world's new cars. In July 2026 Shashua said he would step down as chief executive once a successor is appointed, and the company announced it would run its own robotaxi fleet, targeting a first US city launch in 2027.

Investment case

  • Dominant supplier of front-camera driver assistance chips to carmakers worldwide.
  • Regulation is making automatic emergency braking and similar features mandatory, lifting volumes.
  • Net cash and a profitable core business fund the robotaxi push.

Key risks

  • Chinese carmakers increasingly build their own driver assistance systems.
  • Becoming a robotaxi operator is a large, costly change of business model.
  • Leadership transition after the founder steps down.
