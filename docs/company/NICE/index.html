About NICE Ltd.

Founded in Israel in 1986 to record and analyse calls, and now a leader in cloud contact-centre software through its CXone platform. Scott Russell, formerly of SAP, became CEO in 2025. NICE bought the AI agent start-up Cognigy in 2025. Investors worry that AI chatbots will replace human call-centre agents and the software seats NICE sells for them.

Investment case

  • AI agents could lift what NICE earns per customer.
  • Leader in large enterprise contact centres.
  • Cheap on earnings after a big fall.

Key risks

  • AI may shrink the number of human agents, and with them seat licences.
  • Competition from Genesys, Salesforce and AI-native start-ups.
  • Cloud growth slowed.
