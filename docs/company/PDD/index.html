About PDD Holdings Inc.

Founded in 2015 by Colin Huang as Pinduoduo, a group-buying platform that used social sharing on WeChat to aggregate demand and drive prices down. Chen Lei is co-CEO; the holding company redomiciled to Dublin. PDD runs Pinduoduo in China, focused on value-conscious consumers and agricultural produce, and Temu, the cross-border marketplace that expanded into North America and Europe at extraordinary speed by shipping directly from Chinese manufacturers. Temu's growth has been driven by very heavy advertising spending.

Investment case

  • Extremely capital-light — PDD does not hold inventory or own logistics, so incremental margins are very high.
  • Large net cash position built from years of strong cash generation.
  • Temu built global brand recognition faster than almost any consumer platform in history.

Key risks

  • Temu's economics depend on de minimis import exemptions that regulators in the US and EU are removing.
  • Marketing spend is enormous and the growth stops if it is reduced.
  • Disclosure quality is thin even by Chinese ADR standards, and management rarely engages with investors.
