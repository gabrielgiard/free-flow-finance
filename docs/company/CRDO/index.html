About Credo Technology Group Holding Ltd

Founded in 2008; Bill Brennan has been chief executive since 2013 and the company listed in 2022. Credo makes high-speed connectivity products for data centres. Its best-known product is the active electrical cable — a copper cable with chips in each end that carries data between AI servers and switches more reliably and with less power than the alternatives. It also sells the chips that go inside optical modules and line cards.

Investment case

  • Created the active electrical cable market and still leads it.
  • High margins and no debt.
  • New optical and retimer products widen the opportunity.

Key risks

  • Three or four cloud customers make up most of revenue.
  • Marvell, Broadcom and Astera Labs are all competing for the same sockets.
  • A small company on a very high valuation.
