About Koninklijke Philips N.V.

Founded in Eindhoven in 1891 to make light bulbs, and now a health technology company after selling its lighting, TV and appliance arms. Roy Jakobs has been CEO since 2022. Philips makes MRI and CT scanners, ultrasound, patient monitors and Sonicare toothbrushes, and is still recovering from the 2021 recall of millions of sleep apnoea machines.

Investment case

  • Margins have room to recover as recall costs fade.
  • Strong positions in imaging and patient monitoring.
  • AI tools built into scanners and software.

Key risks

  • Sales in China have been weak.
  • Tariffs on medical equipment between the US, Europe and China.
  • The sleep apnoea recall damaged trust and still brings legal costs.
