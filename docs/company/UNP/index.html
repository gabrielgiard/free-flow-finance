About Union Pacific Corporation

Chartered by Congress in 1862 to build the eastern portion of the first transcontinental railroad. Jim Vena became CEO in 2023. Union Pacific operates around 32,000 route miles across the western two-thirds of the United States, hauling coal, grain, chemicals, industrial products and intermodal containers. Railroads are close to un-replicable — no one is building a competing transcontinental network — which gives the industry unusual structural pricing power.

Investment case

  • The rail network is a genuinely irreplaceable asset with enormous barriers to entry.
  • Rail is substantially more fuel-efficient than trucking, a structural cost and emissions advantage.
  • Precision scheduled railroading has delivered real and durable margin improvement.

Key risks

  • Volumes track industrial production and are cyclical.
  • Coal, historically a major freight category, is in permanent structural decline.
  • Service reliability problems draw regulatory attention and can trigger intervention.
