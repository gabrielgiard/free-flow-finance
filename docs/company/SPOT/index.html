About Spotify Technology S.A.

Founded in 2006 by Daniel Ek and Martin Lorentzon in Stockholm, as a legal alternative to the piracy then devastating the music industry. Ek moved to executive chairman in January 2026, with Gustav Söderström and Alex Norström becoming co-CEOs. Spotify is the largest music streaming service in the world by subscribers. Its structural challenge is that the labels own the music and take roughly two thirds of every euro of revenue, which caps gross margin.

Investment case

  • Largest music streaming subscriber base globally, with scale advantages in licensing negotiations.
  • Price increases have been absorbed with minimal churn, demonstrating real pricing power.
  • Podcasts and audiobooks carry better economics than music because the content costs are lower.

Key risks

  • The three major labels control the catalogue and take the majority of revenue — a permanent structural constraint.
  • Apple and Amazon can subsidise competing services as features of larger ecosystems.
  • Years of heavy podcast investment produced disappointing returns and required painful write-downs.
