About U.S. Bancorp

Traces to the First National Bank of Cincinnati, chartered 1863. Gunjan Kedia became CEO in 2025. U.S. Bancorp is the largest US regional bank, with a branch network across the Midwest and West plus a payments processing business unusual for a bank of its size, providing fee income uncorrelated with lending.

Investment case

  • The payments business gives fee revenue most regional banks lack entirely.
  • Historically among the best returns on equity in US regional banking.
  • Diversified deposit base with a strong regional franchise.

Key risks

  • Regional banks face structural deposit cost pressure from money market alternatives.
  • Commercial real estate exposure is a persistent concern across the sector.
  • The Union Bank integration consumed management attention and capital.
