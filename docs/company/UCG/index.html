About UniCredit S.p.A.

Formed in 1998 from a merger of Italian savings banks and now one of Europe's most profitable large banks. Andrea Orcel, CEO since 2021, cut costs, lifted returns above 15% and has returned most profits to shareholders; he has also built stakes in Germany's Commerzbank and Italy's Banco BPM and Generali. UniCredit has big businesses in Italy, Germany (HypoVereinsbank), Austria and Central and Eastern Europe. Priced in euros from Milan, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • One of the highest returns on equity among Europe's big banks.
  • Distributes close to all of its profit through dividends and buybacks.
  • Excess capital gives options: deals or more buybacks.

Key risks

  • Mergers-and-acquisitions ambitions bring political and execution risk, especially in Germany.
  • Net interest income falls as eurozone rates come down.
  • Italian government bond exposure ties it to Italy's finances.
