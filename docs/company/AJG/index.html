About Arthur J. Gallagher & Co.

Founded in 1927 by Arthur J. Gallagher; his grandson has run it since 1995. Gallagher is the third largest insurance broker globally, built through an unusually consistent programme of acquiring small regional brokerages — dozens a year — and folding them into a common platform. It focuses on middle-market clients rather than the largest multinationals.

Investment case

  • A repeatable acquisition model in a fragmented market with hundreds of remaining targets.
  • Middle-market focus faces less competition than the largest accounts.
  • Broking economics: commission on premium with no underwriting risk.

Key risks

  • Growth depends on continued acquisitions at sensible prices.
  • Integration risk compounds when acquiring dozens of businesses annually.
  • Commission revenue falls if insurance pricing softens.
