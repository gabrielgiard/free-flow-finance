About Circle Internet Group, Inc.

Founded in 2013 by Jeremy Allaire and Sean Neville. Allaire is chief executive. Circle issues USDC, the second-largest dollar stablecoin: a digital token redeemable one-for-one for dollars, backed by cash and short-term Treasury bills. Circle earns the interest on those reserves, sharing a large part with distribution partners, especially Coinbase. It listed on the New York Stock Exchange in June 2025, shortly before the US passed the GENIUS Act, the first federal law for stablecoins.

Investment case

  • The GENIUS Act gives regulated stablecoins legal clarity in the US.
  • USDC circulation has grown quickly as stablecoins spread into payments and trading.
  • A regulated, transparent issuer is well placed to win banks and payment companies.

Key risks

  • Revenue depends on interest rates; rate cuts reduce income directly.
  • A large share of income is paid to Coinbase and other distributors.
  • Competition from Tether, banks and new issuers allowed by the new law.
