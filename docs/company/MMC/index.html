About Marsh & McLennan Companies, Inc.

Founded in 1871 in Chicago. John Doyle has been CEO since 2023. Marsh McLennan is the largest insurance broker in the world, placing risk between companies and insurers and collecting commissions, plus Mercer in employee benefits consulting and Oliver Wyman in management consulting. Broking is an unusually good business: it takes a share of premium without underwriting any risk, so it profits from rising insurance prices without bearing the losses.

Investment case

  • Earns commission on premium without taking underwriting risk — all the upside of hard markets, none of the losses.
  • Highly recurring revenue as insurance programmes renew annually.
  • Genuine expertise barriers in complex commercial and specialty risk placement.

Key risks

  • Commission revenue falls when insurance pricing softens.
  • Consulting revenue is discretionary and cyclical.
  • Regulatory scrutiny of broker compensation and contingent commissions recurs periodically.
