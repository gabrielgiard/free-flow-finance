About Moody's Corporation

Founded in 1909 when John Moody published the first bond ratings manual. Robert Fauber has been CEO since 2021. Moody's is one of the two dominant credit rating agencies, and separately runs Moody's Analytics, which sells risk data, models and software. Regulatory frameworks worldwide reference credit ratings directly, which means issuers cannot practically avoid obtaining one.

Investment case

  • A regulated duopoly with S&P, protected by regulatory reliance on ratings.
  • Moody's Analytics provides recurring subscription revenue that offsets ratings cyclicality.
  • Debt issuance grows structurally over time as global credit markets expand.

Key risks

  • Ratings revenue swings with debt issuance volumes, which are highly rate-sensitive.
  • Periodic regulatory pressure on the issuer-pays model that funds the agencies.
  • The valuation consistently reflects a high-quality premium.
