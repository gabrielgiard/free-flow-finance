About Broadcom Inc.

Traces back to a 1961 HP division; the modern company was assembled by Hock Tan through serial acquisition (LSI, Brocade, CA, Symantec, VMware). Headquartered in Palo Alto. Two very different halves: semiconductors — custom AI accelerators (XPUs) designed with Google, Meta and OpenAI, Tomahawk and Jericho Ethernet switching silicon, wireless chips for the iPhone — and infrastructure software, dominated by VMware Cloud Foundation, which Tan repriced onto subscription contracts after the 2023 takeover.

Investment case

  • The custom-ASIC business is the credible second source to NVIDIA — hyperscalers actively want it to exist.
  • VMware turned a licence business into recurring subscription revenue with software-like margins near 70%.
  • Hock Tan's capital allocation record is one of the best in large-cap technology over the past decade.

Key risks

  • A cautious AI outlook from Broadcom itself helped trigger the June 2026 sector selloff — guidance is a market-moving event.
  • Customer concentration in custom silicon is severe; losing one hyperscaler design win is a multi-billion-dollar revenue hole.
  • Around $55B of net debt from the VMware deal is manageable but limits flexibility if rates rise further.
