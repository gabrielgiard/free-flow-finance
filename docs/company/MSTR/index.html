About Strategy Inc. (MicroStrategy)

Founded in 1989 by Michael Saylor as a business intelligence software company. Phong Le is CEO; Saylor is executive chairman and drives strategy. Since 2020 the company has pursued an explicit treasury policy of acquiring and holding bitcoin, funded through convertible notes, preferred stock and at-the-market equity issuance. It renamed itself Strategy in 2025. The original enterprise analytics software business still exists and generates modest revenue, but the share price behaves almost entirely as a leveraged claim on bitcoin rather than as a software equity.

Investment case

  • Provides leveraged bitcoin exposure through a regular brokerage account, which some mandates cannot obtain directly.
  • Convertible note issuance has historically been executed at favourable terms during periods of high volatility.
  • The software business, while small, generates cash that services some of the financing.

Key risks

  • A discounted cash flow on the software business tells you almost nothing about the share price, and we want to be explicit about that.
  • The premium to net asset value swings wildly and has at times exceeded 100%, which is a pure sentiment premium.
  • Leverage cuts both ways — a sustained bitcoin drawdown creates real financing pressure.
