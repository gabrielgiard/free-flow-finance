About Fomento Económico Mexicano, S.A.B. de C.V. (FEMSA)

Founded in 1890 as a Monterrey brewery and now Mexico's largest retail group. José Antonio Fernández Garza-Lagüera became CEO in 2024. FEMSA runs OXXO, Latin America's largest convenience store chain with over 23,000 stores, pharmacies, the Spin payments app, and a large stake in Coca-Cola FEMSA, the world's biggest Coca-Cola bottler. One US-listed share represents ten Mexican units.

Investment case

  • OXXO keeps opening stores and dominates Mexican convenience retail.
  • Spin could become a large digital bank.
  • Simplification: non-core stakes have been sold.

Key risks

  • Heavy investment keeps free cash flow low.
  • Mexico's economy and peso.
  • US trade policy toward Mexico.
