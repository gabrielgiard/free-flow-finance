About Amgen Inc.

Founded in 1980 as Applied Molecular Genetics in Thousand Oaks, California, one of the original biotechnology companies. Robert Bradway has been CEO since 2012. Amgen's portfolio spans Repatha for cholesterol, Prolia and Evenity for osteoporosis, Tezspire for asthma, the oncology drug Blincyto, and a large biosimilars business. Its 2023 acquisition of Horizon Therapeutics added Tepezza for thyroid eye disease and Krystexxa for gout. MariTide, a monthly injectable obesity drug, is the company's most-watched pipeline asset.

Investment case

  • MariTide's monthly dosing would be a genuine differentiator against weekly GLP-1 injections.
  • Rare disease assets from Horizon carry high prices and limited competition.
  • Biosimilars provide a growing revenue stream that benefits as branded biologics lose exclusivity.

Key risks

  • Around $52B of net debt from the Horizon deal is high and constrains further business development.
  • Prolia and other mature products face biosimilar erosion of their own.
  • MariTide is a binary event — the obesity market is unforgiving and Lilly and Novo are far ahead.
