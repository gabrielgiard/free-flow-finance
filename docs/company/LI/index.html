About Li Auto Inc.

Founded in 2015 by Li Xiang, a serial entrepreneur who previously founded the car website Autohome. Li is chairman and chief executive. Li Auto built its business on extended-range electric SUVs, which carry a small petrol engine that recharges the battery, removing range anxiety for families. That made it the first Chinese electric vehicle start-up to turn consistently profitable. It has since moved into fully electric models, including the Mega people mover and the i-series SUVs.

Investment case

  • The extended-range formula gave it the best profitability of China's electric vehicle start-ups.
  • A large net cash position cushions the price war.
  • Strong brand among Chinese families for large SUVs.

Key risks

  • China's car price war keeps pressing margins down.
  • The shift to fully electric models puts it head to head with Tesla, BYD and Xiaomi.
  • Sales fell in 2025 as competition caught up with its formula.
