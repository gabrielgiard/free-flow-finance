About ServiceNow, Inc.

Founded in 2004 by Fred Luddy, a former CTO who wanted to fix corporate IT ticketing. Bill McDermott, previously CEO of SAP, has run it since 2019 from Santa Clara. ServiceNow started in IT service management and expanded into a general workflow platform covering HR, customer service, security operations and, increasingly, AI agent orchestration. Its Now Assist products embed generative AI into those workflows. Customers are almost entirely large enterprises and governments, on multi-year subscription contracts with unusually high renewal rates. Share count and price here reflect the stock split completed in 2026; the stock has also fallen sharply from its 2025 high.

Investment case

  • Renewal rates above 98% are among the best in enterprise software — the platform becomes the connective tissue of internal operations.
  • Workflow orchestration is arguably where AI agents create demand rather than destroy it, since agents need a system to run inside.
  • Consistent 20%+ subscription growth at scale with expanding free cash flow margins.

Key risks

  • One of the most expensive large-cap software names on any multiple, which leaves no room for a growth miss.
  • US federal government exposure is meaningful and subject to budget politics.
  • Large-deal timing can push revenue between quarters and create sharp share price reactions.
