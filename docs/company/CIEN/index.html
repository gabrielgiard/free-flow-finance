About Ciena Corporation

Founded in 1992. Gary Smith has been chief executive since 2001. Ciena makes the optical networking equipment that sends data over fibre across cities, continents and oceans; its WaveLogic technology is the industry benchmark for how much data one fibre can carry. Cloud companies, which need to connect AI data centres to each other, have overtaken telecom operators as its most important customers.

Investment case

  • AI data centres need far more capacity between sites, which is Ciena's core market.
  • A technology lead in coherent optics, with Huawei shut out of many markets.
  • Moving into optics inside the data centre opens a larger market.

Key risks

  • A handful of cloud customers account for a large share of sales.
  • Telecom operator spending is slow and lumpy.
  • The valuation has risen a long way on AI expectations.
