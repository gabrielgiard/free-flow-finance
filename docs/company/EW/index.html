About Edwards Lifesciences Corporation

Traces to Miles Lowell Edwards, who built the first artificial heart valve in 1958, and separated from Baxter in 2000. Bernard Zovighian has been CEO since 2023. Edwards dominates transcatheter aortic valve replacement — replacing a heart valve via catheter rather than open-heart surgery, which makes the procedure available to patients too frail for surgery.

Investment case

  • Clear leadership in transcatheter aortic valves with strong clinical evidence behind it.
  • The eligible patient population keeps expanding as trials extend into lower-risk groups.
  • Mitral and tricuspid programmes open genuinely large new markets.

Key risks

  • Aortic valve growth has decelerated as the initial market matures.
  • Medtronic and Abbott compete directly and are investing heavily.
  • Hospital procedure capacity and staffing limit how fast volumes can grow.
