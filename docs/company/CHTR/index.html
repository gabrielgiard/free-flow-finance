About Charter Communications, Inc.

Founded in 1993 and rebuilt after bankruptcy under John Malone's influence, acquiring Time Warner Cable in 2016. Christopher Winfrey has been CEO since 2022. Charter is the second largest US cable operator, selling broadband, video and mobile service under the Spectrum brand to around 57 million passings. Its mobile business resells Verizon's network, which has grown quickly by bundling with broadband at aggressive prices.

Investment case

  • Broadband infrastructure has high replacement cost and serves as an essential service.
  • Mobile bundling has been genuinely effective at reducing broadband churn.
  • Extremely aggressive share repurchases have reduced the share count dramatically.

Key risks

  • Broadband subscriber losses to fixed wireless and fibre overbuilders have been persistent.
  • Very high leverage — among the most indebted large US companies relative to earnings.
  • Video subscribers decline every quarter as cord-cutting continues.
