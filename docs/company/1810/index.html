About Xiaomi Corporation

Founded in 2010 by Lei Jun, who remains CEO. Xiaomi is the world's third-largest smartphone maker and sells a huge range of connected devices, from TVs to rice cookers, through its own stores and online. In 2024 it launched electric cars; the SU7 sedan and YU7 SUV became hits in China and its car business turned profitable in 2025. Priced in Hong Kong dollars, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • The car business has grown faster than almost anyone expected.
  • A loyal ecosystem of hundreds of millions of users.
  • Large net cash.

Key risks

  • Memory chip costs are squeezing phone margins.
  • The Chinese EV price war.
  • US sanctions risk.
