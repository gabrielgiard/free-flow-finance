About Deutsche Telekom AG

Created in 1995 when Germany privatised its state telephone company, and Europe's largest telecom operator; the German state still owns about 28%. Tim Höttges has been CEO since 2014. It controls T-Mobile US, which supplies over 60% of profit. Because it consolidates all of T-Mobile US but owns only about half of it, net debt here includes the value of the other T-Mobile shareholders. Priced in euros from Frankfurt, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • T-Mobile US keeps taking customers from rivals.
  • Steady German business and rising dividends.
  • Buybacks at both parent and T-Mobile.

Key risks

  • Value depends heavily on T-Mobile US.
  • European telecom competition and regulation.
  • High debt including leases.
