About CRH plc

Formed in Ireland in 1970 by merging Cement Limited and Roadstone. Jim Mintern became chief executive at the start of 2025. CRH is the largest building materials company in North America, supplying crushed stone, cement, asphalt and ready-mixed concrete and paving a large share of American roads; it earns about three-quarters of its profit in the United States. It moved its main stock listing from London to New York in 2023 and joined the S&P 500 in 2025.

Investment case

  • Quarries are local monopolies: stone is too heavy to haul far.
  • US government spending on roads and bridges runs for years.
  • A steady programme of buying small local competitors.

Key risks

  • Construction activity follows interest rates and the economy.
  • Housing and commercial building have been weak.
  • Energy and labour costs.
