About Chubb Limited

Chubb traces to 1882 in New York; the current company was formed when ACE acquired Chubb in 2016 and took its name. Evan Greenberg has been CEO since 2004. Chubb is the largest publicly traded property and casualty insurer in the world, writing commercial and high-net-worth personal insurance across some 54 countries. It is known for underwriting discipline — willingness to shrink premium volume rather than write business at inadequate rates.

Investment case

  • Consistent underwriting profitability across cycles, which is rare in property and casualty insurance.
  • Global footprint gives access to markets most US insurers cannot reach.
  • Rising interest rates increase investment income on the float without any underwriting change.

Key risks

  • Catastrophe exposure is inherent and climate trends make historical loss data less predictive.
  • Commercial insurance pricing is cyclical and has passed its peak in several lines.
  • Reserve adequacy for long-tail liability lines is difficult for outsiders to verify.
