About Veeva Systems Inc.

Founded in 2007 by Peter Gassner, previously at Salesforce, who still runs it. Veeva sells cloud software exclusively to life sciences companies — customer relationship management for pharmaceutical sales teams, plus clinical trial and regulatory document management. Building only for one heavily regulated industry means the software handles compliance requirements generic vendors do not attempt.

Investment case

  • Near-total focus on life sciences creates domain depth competitors cannot easily match.
  • Regulated document workflows have very high switching costs once validated.
  • Almost every large pharmaceutical company is already a customer, and they expand steadily.

Key risks

  • A relatively finite customer universe — there are only so many large drug companies.
  • Salesforce, its former platform partner, has become a direct competitor.
  • Pharmaceutical R&D budget pressure flows through to software spend.
