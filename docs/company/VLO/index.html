About Valero Energy Corporation

Created in 1980 and built up through acquisitions into the largest independent oil refiner in the world. Lane Riggs has been chief executive since 2023. Valero turns crude oil into petrol, diesel and jet fuel at refineries concentrated on the US Gulf Coast, and does not produce oil itself. It is also a large maker of ethanol and, through a joint venture, of renewable diesel from used cooking oil and animal fats. Its plants are among the most efficient in the industry.

Investment case

  • Low-cost, complex refineries that can run cheap grades of crude.
  • Refinery closures elsewhere have tightened fuel supply and lifted margins.
  • Returns most of its cash to shareholders.

Key risks

  • Refining margins are highly cyclical and currently well above normal.
  • Long-term fuel demand faces electric vehicles and efficiency gains.
  • Renewable fuels depend on government credits.
