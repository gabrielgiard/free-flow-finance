About Shopify Inc.

Founded in 2006 in Ottawa by Tobias Lütke, Daniel Weinand and Scott Lake after they failed to find good software for their snowboard shop. Lütke is still CEO. Shopify provides the commerce infrastructure behind millions of merchants: storefronts, checkout, payments through Shopify Payments, capital lending, shipping, point-of-sale hardware for physical retail, and Shop Pay. Its enterprise tier, Shopify Plus, has won large brands away from legacy platforms. Merchant Solutions — the take-rate businesses — now generate far more revenue than subscriptions.

Investment case

  • Revenue scales with merchant gross merchandise volume rather than seat count, so Shopify grows as its customers grow.
  • Payments attach rate keeps rising, and payments is where the economics actually are.
  • Moving upmarket into enterprise brands raises average revenue per merchant substantially.

Key risks

  • Consumer discretionary spending is directly exposed to the 2026 oil shock and tighter financial conditions.
  • Amazon, and increasingly TikTok and Instagram commerce, compete for the same merchant relationships.
  • The multiple is high enough that any deceleration in gross merchandise volume gets punished hard.
