About Sony Group Corporation

Founded in 1946 by Masaru Ibuka and Akio Morita as Tokyo Tsushin Kogyo. Hiroki Totoki became chief executive in April 2025. Sony has transformed from a hardware maker into an entertainment company: PlayStation is its largest business, Sony Music is the world's second-largest record company, and Sony Pictures makes films and television. It is also the world's largest maker of image sensors for smartphone cameras. It spun off its financial services arm, Sony Financial Group, in October 2025 to focus on entertainment and technology.

Investment case

  • PlayStation's installed base and network subscriptions produce recurring revenue.
  • A broad intellectual property catalogue across games, music and film that can be reused across formats.
  • Global leader in smartphone image sensors.

Key risks

  • Console cycles are lumpy and hardware margins are thin.
  • Rising memory and component costs squeeze hardware profits.
  • Film and television earnings are volatile.
