About T-Mobile US, Inc.

Traces to VoiceStream Wireless, acquired by Deutsche Telekom in 2001. Srini Gopalan became CEO in November 2025, succeeding Mike Sievert. The 2020 Sprint merger transformed T-Mobile from a distant third to a genuine scale competitor, and crucially handed it a deep mid-band spectrum position that let it build 5G coverage faster than either rival. It has taken subscriber share consistently ever since, and the 'Un-carrier' positioning still resonates.

Investment case

  • The best mid-band 5G spectrum position in the US, which translated into a real coverage lead.
  • Consistently the strongest subscriber growth of the three major carriers.
  • Sprint merger synergies have exceeded original targets.

Key risks

  • Sprint integration synergies are largely realised, so that earnings tailwind is ending.
  • Growth increasingly depends on fixed wireless and rural expansion rather than core share gains.
  • Deutsche Telekom's majority ownership limits governance independence.
