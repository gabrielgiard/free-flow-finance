About Ping An Insurance (Group) Company of China

Founded in Shenzhen in 1988 by Ma Mingzhe as one of China's first joint-stock insurers. Xie Yonglin is co-CEO and president. Ping An is China's largest insurer by market value and a financial conglomerate, owning Ping An Bank, an asset manager and health and technology businesses. Priced from its Hong Kong shares, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • Trades close to book value with a high dividend yield.
  • Life insurance new business is recovering after a long slump.
  • Scale and data in health and financial services.

Key risks

  • Chinese property exposure through investments and the bank.
  • Falling Chinese bond yields squeeze life insurance returns.
  • Conglomerate complexity makes earnings hard to read.
