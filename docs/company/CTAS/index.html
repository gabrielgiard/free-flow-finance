About Cintas Corporation

Traces to 1929, when Richard 'Doc' Farmer began collecting and cleaning shop rags in Cincinnati; his grandson Richard Farmer built it into a uniform company. Todd Schneider has been chief executive since 2021. Cintas rents and cleans work uniforms for more than a million businesses and supplies first aid, safety and fire protection services, delivered on regular routes.

Investment case

  • Recurring route-based revenue with high customer retention.
  • Steady margin expansion from route density and technology.
  • Long runway to win businesses that do not yet outsource uniforms.

Key risks

  • Growth is tied to US employment.
  • A high valuation for a services business.
  • Labour and fuel costs.
