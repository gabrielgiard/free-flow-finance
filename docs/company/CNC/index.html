About Centene Corporation

Founded in 1984 by Elizabeth Brinn as a single Wisconsin health plan. Sarah London has been CEO since 2022. Centene is the largest US Medicaid managed care organisation, administering government health programmes for low-income populations across most states, plus a substantial marketplace insurance business. Revenue depends almost entirely on government contracts and reimbursement rates.

Investment case

  • Scale leader in Medicaid managed care, an area states continue to outsource.
  • Marketplace enrolment has grown substantially with enhanced subsidies.
  • Trades at a low multiple relative to the broader managed care group.

Key risks

  • Medicaid redeterminations left a sicker remaining membership, pressuring margins badly.
  • State reimbursement rates have lagged actual medical cost trends.
  • Expiry of enhanced marketplace subsidies is a direct and dated risk to enrolment.
