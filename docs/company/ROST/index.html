About Ross Stores, Inc.

Traces to 1950 when Morris Ross opened a department store in San Bruno, California; investors turned it into an off-price chain in 1982. Jim Conroy became chief executive in 2025. Ross runs Ross Dress for Less and dd's DISCOUNTS, selling brand-name clothing and home goods at 20% to 70% below department store prices by buying excess and end-of-season inventory. It operates around 2,200 stores.

Investment case

  • Off-price retail keeps winning share from department stores.
  • Abundant excess inventory across the industry helps it buy cheaply.
  • A net cash balance sheet and steady store growth.

Key risks

  • Tariffs raise the cost of imported goods.
  • Lower-income shoppers are under pressure.
  • TJX is a larger, stronger competitor.
