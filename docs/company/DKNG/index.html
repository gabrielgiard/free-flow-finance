About DraftKings Inc.

Founded in 2012 by Jason Robins, Matt Kalish and Paul Liberman as a fantasy sports site. Robins is chief executive. When the US Supreme Court allowed states to legalise sports betting in 2018, DraftKings became one of the two leading online sportsbooks alongside FanDuel, and it also runs online casino games in the handful of states that allow them. Its shares have fallen as prediction markets, which offer sports contracts nationwide under federal rules, emerged as a new kind of competitor.

Investment case

  • One of two clear leaders in a market that is still young.
  • Marketing costs fall as states mature, lifting margins.
  • More states may legalise sports betting and online casino.

Key risks

  • Prediction markets operate in every state without the same taxes.
  • States keep raising taxes on betting revenue.
  • Results swing with sports outcomes.
