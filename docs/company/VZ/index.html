About Verizon Communications Inc.

Formed in 2000 from Bell Atlantic, itself created in the 1983 AT&T breakup. Dan Schulman, previously CEO of PayPal, took over in October 2025. Verizon operates the largest US wireless network by subscribers and has historically positioned on network quality at premium prices. It has been slower than competitors to grow subscribers and is pushing fixed wireless access — using spare 5G capacity to deliver home broadband — as its main growth vector.

Investment case

  • Network quality reputation supports premium pricing and low churn among high-value customers.
  • Fixed wireless broadband monetises existing spectrum with very little incremental capital.
  • The dividend yield is among the highest in the S&P 500 and has been raised annually for years.

Key risks

  • The heaviest debt burden of the three major US carriers.
  • Consistently weaker postpaid phone subscriber growth than T-Mobile.
  • Very large spectrum purchases have not yet translated into commensurate returns.
