About Medtronic plc

Founded in 1949 by Earl Bakken in a Minneapolis garage, repairing medical equipment before building the first wearable external pacemaker. Geoffrey Martha has been CEO since 2020. Medtronic is the largest pure-play medical device company in the world, spanning cardiac rhythm management, cardiac ablation, surgical robotics and neuromodulation. Its diabetes business was listed separately as MiniMed in March 2026, and in September 2026 Medtronic launched an exchange offer to complete the separation. Its Irish domicile dates from the 2015 Covidien acquisition.

Investment case

  • Enormous installed base and long-standing surgeon relationships create real switching friction.
  • Pulsed field ablation is a genuinely new cardiac technology where Medtronic entered early, and cardiac ablation revenue rose 88% in the first quarter of fiscal 2027.
  • Procedure volumes have recovered well and the demographic tailwind is durable.

Key risks

  • Growth has trailed peers such as Boston Scientific and Stryker for several years.
  • The Hugo surgical robot is far behind Intuitive's da Vinci in adoption.
  • Diabetes has lost share to Dexcom and Abbott in continuous glucose monitoring.
