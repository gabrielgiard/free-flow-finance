About Medtronic plc

Founded in 1949 by Earl Bakken in a Minneapolis garage, repairing medical equipment before building the first wearable external pacemaker. Geoffrey Martha has been CEO since 2020. Medtronic is the largest pure-play medical device company in the world, spanning cardiac rhythm management, surgical robotics, diabetes technology and neuromodulation. Its Irish domicile dates from the 2015 Covidien acquisition.

Investment case

  • Enormous installed base and long-standing surgeon relationships create real switching friction.
  • Pulsed field ablation is a genuinely new cardiac technology where Medtronic entered early.
  • Procedure volumes have recovered well and the demographic tailwind is durable.

Key risks

  • Growth has trailed peers such as Boston Scientific and Stryker for several years.
  • The Hugo surgical robot is far behind Intuitive's da Vinci in adoption.
  • Diabetes has lost share to Dexcom and Abbott in continuous glucose monitoring.
