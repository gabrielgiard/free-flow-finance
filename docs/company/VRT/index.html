About Vertiv Holdings Co

Originally the Emerson Network Power division, founded in 1965 and separated in 2016, then listed via a special purpose acquisition company in 2020. Giordano Albertazzi is CEO, headquartered in Westerville, Ohio. Vertiv makes the physical infrastructure that keeps data centres running: uninterruptible power supplies, busways, switchgear, precision cooling, and — most importantly for AI — liquid cooling systems. High-density AI racks generate too much heat for air cooling, which has made liquid cooling a requirement rather than an option and put Vertiv in a strong position.

Investment case

  • Liquid cooling is mandatory for high-density AI racks, and Vertiv is one of very few suppliers with scale.
  • Order backlog has grown faster than revenue, indicating demand well ahead of delivery capability.
  • Services and spare parts on installed equipment provide recurring revenue.

Key risks

  • Directly exposed to any slowdown in data centre construction, which is the crowded consensus trade.
  • Competition from Schneider Electric, Eaton and specialised cooling manufacturers is increasing.
  • The stock is priced for sustained high growth and would de-rate quickly on an order slowdown.
