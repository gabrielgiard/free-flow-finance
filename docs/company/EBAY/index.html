About eBay Inc.

Founded in 1995 by Pierre Omidyar as an online auction site. Jamie Iannone has been chief executive since 2020. eBay is a marketplace where around 130 million buyers purchase from millions of sellers; it holds no stock itself and earns fees and advertising revenue. Under Iannone it has concentrated on categories where it is strongest — collectibles, car parts, refurbished goods and luxury items — and added authentication and live selling.

Investment case

  • Focus categories and advertising are growing faster than the rest of the site.
  • An asset-light model that turns a large share of revenue into cash.
  • Steady buybacks shrink the share count each year.

Key risks

  • The number of active buyers has barely grown in years.
  • Amazon, Temu and specialist resale sites compete for the same shoppers.
  • Much of what it sells is discretionary.
