About Philip Morris International

Spun off from Altria in 2008 to hold the international tobacco business, headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut. Jacek Olczak is CEO. The strategy is an explicit transition away from cigarettes: IQOS heated tobacco, now the leading smoke-free product in dozens of markets, and ZYN nicotine pouches, acquired through Swedish Match in 2022, which have grown extraordinarily fast in the United States. Smoke-free products already generate around 40% of revenue and carry higher margins than combustible cigarettes. It also sells Marlboro outside the US.

Investment case

  • ZYN volume growth has been exceptional and the category has better economics than cigarettes.
  • IQOS gives a credible path to converting smokers rather than simply managing decline.
  • Extremely strong pricing power — tobacco price increases have historically outpaced volume declines every year.

Key risks

  • Regulatory risk is permanent and can arrive suddenly, including outright category bans.
  • Roughly $45B of net debt from the Swedish Match acquisition needs servicing.
  • Many institutional investors are excluded from tobacco by mandate, which caps the buyer base.
