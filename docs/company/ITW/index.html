About Illinois Tool Works Inc.

Founded in 1912 in Chicago. Christopher O'Herlihy has been CEO since 2023. Illinois Tool Works operates around eighty decentralised business units making engineered fasteners, welding equipment, food equipment, automotive components and testing instruments. Its 80/20 management principle — concentrating effort on the 20% of products and customers generating 80% of profit — has produced consistently high margins across a very diverse portfolio.

Investment case

  • The 80/20 principle has delivered operating margins above 25%, exceptional for diversified industrials.
  • Decentralised structure keeps business units close to their customers.
  • Long record of dividend increases spanning six decades.

Key risks

  • Exposed to industrial production and automotive build rates, both cyclical.
  • Organic growth has been consistently low single digit.
  • Automotive OEM exposure carries EV transition risk.
