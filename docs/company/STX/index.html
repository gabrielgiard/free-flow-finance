About Seagate Technology Holdings plc

Founded in 1979 by Al Shugart and Finis Conner. Dave Mosley has been CEO since 2017. Seagate makes hard disk drives, and although solid state storage replaced spinning disks in consumer devices years ago, HDDs remain far cheaper per terabyte at scale — which is why cloud providers still buy them in enormous volumes for bulk storage. AI workloads generate vast quantities of data that has to live somewhere affordable.

Investment case

  • Cost per terabyte remains dramatically below flash, which keeps HDDs essential for cloud bulk storage.
  • Effectively a duopoly with Western Digital, and both have shown pricing discipline.
  • HAMR technology raises areal density substantially and Seagate is furthest along.

Key risks

  • Structurally declining unit volumes, offset only by rising capacity per drive.
  • Flash prices falling faster than expected would compress the cost advantage.
  • Very high customer concentration among a few hyperscalers.
