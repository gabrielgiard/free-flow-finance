About Mizuho Financial Group, Inc.

Created in 2000 by merging three Japanese banks, Dai-Ichi Kangyo, Fuji and the Industrial Bank of Japan, and one of Japan's three megabanks. Masahiro Kihara has been CEO since 2022. Mizuho lends to most of Japan's large companies and runs securities, trust and asset management arms; its US securities business has grown into a real Wall Street player. Japan's return to positive interest rates is lifting its income for the first time in decades.

Investment case

  • Rising Japanese interest rates widen lending margins after 25 years near zero.
  • Corporate governance reform in Japan is pushing banks to sell cross-shareholdings and buy back stock.
  • US investment banking expansion diversifies earnings.

Key risks

  • If the Bank of Japan stops raising rates, the margin tailwind fades.
  • Large holdings of Japanese government bonds lose value as yields rise.
  • History of IT outages and operational problems.
