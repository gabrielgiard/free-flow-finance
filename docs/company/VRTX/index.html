About Vertex Pharmaceuticals

Founded in 1989 by Joshua Boger in Cambridge, Massachusetts, with a then-radical commitment to structure-based rational drug design. Reshma Kewalramani has been CEO since 2020. Vertex effectively owns the cystic fibrosis market with Trikafta and its successor Alyftrek, treating the underlying protein defect rather than symptoms. It has diversified into Casgevy, the first approved CRISPR gene-editing therapy, developed with CRISPR Therapeutics for sickle cell disease, and Journavx, a non-opioid painkiller approved in 2025 for acute pain. In September 2026 it completed the roughly $10B acquisition of Crinetics Pharmaceuticals, adding Palsonify for acromegaly and atumelnant, in development for congenital adrenal hyperplasia and Cushing's syndrome, as a rare endocrine franchise.

Investment case

  • A near-monopoly in cystic fibrosis with pricing power and patients who stay on therapy for life.
  • Journavx addresses acute pain without opioid addiction risk, which is a very large market with obvious policy support.
  • Large net cash position funds development without dilution.

Key risks

  • Extreme concentration — cystic fibrosis is the overwhelming majority of revenue.
  • Casgevy uptake has been slow because the treatment process is complex and expensive to administer.
  • Cystic fibrosis patent protection eventually erodes and the diversification has to work before then.
