About Cameco Corporation

Formed in 1988 by merging two government-owned companies and later privatised. Tim Gitzel has been chief executive since 2011. Cameco is one of the world's largest uranium producers, mining the exceptionally rich deposits of northern Saskatchewan, and it refines and converts uranium into reactor fuel. It also owns 49% of Westinghouse, which designs and services nuclear reactors, alongside Brookfield. Interest in nuclear power to supply data centres has transformed its prospects.

Investment case

  • Governments and technology companies are backing new nuclear plants.
  • The highest-grade uranium mines in the world, in a stable country.
  • Westinghouse stands to gain from every new reactor built to its design.

Key risks

  • The share price assumes a nuclear revival that will take many years to build.
  • Mining setbacks have repeatedly cut production.
  • Its Kazakh joint venture depends on transport routes through Russia.
