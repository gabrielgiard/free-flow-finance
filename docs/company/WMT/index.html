About Walmart Inc.

Founded in 1962 by Sam Walton in Rogers, Arkansas, still headquartered in nearby Bentonville. Doug McMillon is CEO. Walmart is the largest company in the world by revenue, serving roughly 250 million customers weekly across more than 10,500 stores. It crossed a $1T market capitalisation on 3 February 2026. Beyond retail, it has built three high-margin businesses that are changing the profit mix entirely: Walmart Connect advertising, Walmart Marketplace for third-party sellers, and Walmart+ membership. Sam's Club operates as a separate warehouse format.

Investment case

  • Advertising, marketplace and membership carry far higher margins than retail and are growing much faster than the base business.
  • In an inflationary environment consumers trade down to Walmart, so it gains share exactly when others struggle.
  • Grocery is roughly 60% of US sales, which makes traffic remarkably stable through downturns.

Key risks

  • Retail operating margins are around 4%, so there is very little cushion for execution error.
  • The 2026 oil shock raises both logistics costs and consumer stress simultaneously.
  • The valuation has re-rated substantially and now embeds continued high-margin revenue mix shift.
