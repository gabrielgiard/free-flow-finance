About Nu Holdings Ltd.

Founded in 2013 by David Vélez, Cristina Junqueira and Edward Wible, starting with a purple no-fee credit card in Brazil. Vélez is chief executive. Nubank has grown into one of the largest digital banks in the world, with more than 100 million customers across Brazil, Mexico and Colombia. It has no branches, so its cost to serve each customer is a fraction of a traditional Brazilian bank's, and it uses that advantage to cross-sell credit cards, personal loans, investments and insurance.

Investment case

  • Cost to serve is a fraction of incumbent banks', which lets it profitably bank people they ignore.
  • Already the primary bank for a large share of Brazilian adults, so cross-selling has a big base to work on.
  • Mexico and Colombia repeat the playbook in markets where banking penetration is even lower.

Key risks

  • Unsecured consumer lending in Brazil carries high and cyclical credit losses.
  • Brazilian interest rates and the real swing earnings in dollar terms.
  • Incumbent banks and fintechs are copying the model, so customer acquisition may get harder.
