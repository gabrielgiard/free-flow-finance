About Atlassian Corporation

Founded in 2002 in Sydney by Mike Cannon-Brookes and Scott Farquhar, funded initially on credit cards. Cannon-Brookes is now sole CEO. Atlassian makes Jira and Confluence, the issue tracking and documentation tools that a large share of software teams run on. Its unusual history is that it grew for years with essentially no sales force, relying on developers adopting the tools themselves.

Investment case

  • Jira is deeply embedded in engineering workflows and migrating off it is genuinely painful.
  • Bottom-up adoption keeps customer acquisition costs far below enterprise software norms.
  • The cloud migration from server licences is largely complete and lifted recurring revenue.

Key risks

  • Growth depends heavily on software engineering headcount, which has been cut across the industry.
  • Linear, Notion and others compete for the same teams with more modern interfaces.
  • Free tiers mean a large share of users generate no revenue.
