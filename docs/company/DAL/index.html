About Delta Air Lines, Inc.

Began in the 1920s as a crop-dusting operation in the American South and started carrying passengers in 1929. Ed Bastian has been chief executive since 2016. Delta is the most profitable US airline, with hubs in Atlanta, Detroit, Minneapolis, New York and elsewhere. More than half its revenue now comes from premium cabins, its loyalty programme and a co-branded credit card with American Express, rather than from ordinary economy seats. Unusually, it also owns an oil refinery.

Investment case

  • Premium and loyalty revenue is more stable and more profitable than economy fares.
  • The American Express partnership pays it several billion dollars a year.
  • Debt has fallen back to investment-grade levels.

Key risks

  • Airlines are exposed to fuel prices, recessions and shocks.
  • Labour costs have risen sharply.
  • Corporate and international travel are sensitive to the economy.
