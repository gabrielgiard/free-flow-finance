About Ares Management Corporation

Founded in 1997 by Tony Ressler, John Kissick and others, starting with credit investing in Los Angeles. Michael Arougheti has been chief executive since 2018. Ares is one of the largest alternative asset managers, best known for direct lending to mid-sized companies, and also invests in private equity, real estate and infrastructure. It manages more than $500 billion and expanded in real estate through the 2025 acquisition of GCP International.

Investment case

  • Leader in private credit, which is taking share from bank lending.
  • Fee-related earnings come largely from long-term locked-up capital.
  • Wealth management channels are a fast-growing source of new money.

Key risks

  • Private credit has attracted huge inflows, raising the risk of weaker lending standards.
  • A recession would test loan portfolios.
  • Regulators are looking more closely at private credit.
