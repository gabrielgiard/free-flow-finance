About Tencent Holdings Limited

Founded in 1998 in Shenzhen by Pony Ma and four classmates, initially building an instant messaging service. Ma is still chairman and CEO. Tencent operates WeChat, which has over 1.3 billion users and functions as messaging, payments, social feed and app platform simultaneously — there is no Western equivalent. It is the largest video game company in the world by revenue, owning Riot Games and Supercell and holding stakes in Epic Games and many others. It also runs fintech through WeChat Pay, cloud services, music streaming, and a very large portfolio of minority stakes in listed and private companies.

Investment case

  • WeChat is the most complete consumer platform in the world and is effectively unregulated as a competitor within China.
  • The investment portfolio holds tens of billions in listed stakes that are not fully reflected in the valuation.
  • Games are a high-margin, cash-generative business with strong intellectual property.

Key risks

  • Chinese regulatory policy toward technology platforms can change abruptly and has done so before.
  • US listing and delisting risk affects ADR holders specifically.
  • Chinese consumer spending has been weak and advertising revenue follows it.
