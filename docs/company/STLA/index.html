About Stellantis N.V.

Created in 2021 by merging Fiat Chrysler Automobiles with France's PSA Group. Antonio Filosa became CEO in 2025 after a difficult period. Headquartered in Hoofddorp, Netherlands. Stellantis owns fourteen brands including Jeep, Ram, Dodge, Chrysler, Peugeot, Citroën, Opel, Fiat, Alfa Romeo and Maserati. Jeep and Ram in North America generate most of the profit. The company struggled badly in 2024 and 2025 with US inventory build-up, aggressive pricing and market share losses, and is in the middle of an operational reset.

Investment case

  • Trades at a very low valuation after a near-60% share price fall in 2026, so expectations are minimal.
  • Fourteen brands and a broad geographic footprint provide multiple routes to recovery.
  • South American operations are genuinely strong and often overlooked.

Key risks

  • Quality problems have produced a record recall year in 2026, including 1.5 million Ram trucks and 201,976 Jeeps, undermining the brand repair the turnaround depends on.
  • European emissions regulations require electric vehicle sales that are currently loss-making.
  • Brand proliferation creates complexity and marketing costs that peers do not carry.
