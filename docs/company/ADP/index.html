About Automatic Data Processing, Inc.

Founded in 1949 by Henry Taub as a manual payroll processing service. Maria Black has been CEO since 2023. ADP runs payroll for a very large share of American workers, and its monthly employment report is watched as an economic indicator in its own right. Beyond payroll it sells human resources outsourcing, benefits administration and compliance services.

Investment case

  • Payroll is the single stickiest enterprise system there is — switching risks paying people incorrectly.
  • Earns float income on client funds held between collection and disbursement, which rises with rates.
  • Scale advantages in navigating tax and compliance rules across every jurisdiction.

Key risks

  • Revenue tracks employment levels, so a recession reduces both clients and employees per client.
  • Workday and Paycom compete hard at the larger end of the market.
  • Growth is steady but structurally low single digit.
