About Iberdrola, S.A.

Formed in 1992 by merging two Spanish utilities, Iberduero (founded 1901) and Hidroeléctrica Española. Ignacio Sánchez Galán has led it since 2001 and turned it into one of the world's largest utilities and wind power producers. Iberdrola owns power networks and renewables in Spain, the UK (ScottishPower), the US (Avangrid) and Brazil, and is shifting most investment toward networks. Priced in euros from Madrid, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • Networks investment gives regulated growth.
  • Large renewable portfolio.
  • A steady dividend.

Key risks

  • Heavy investment keeps free cash flow thin.
  • Interest rates raise financing costs.
  • Regulatory and political risk in several countries.
