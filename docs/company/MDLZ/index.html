About Mondelez International, Inc.

Created in 2012 when Kraft Foods split, with Mondelez taking the global snacks business. Dirk Van de Put has been CEO since 2017. The portfolio is built on Oreo, Cadbury, Milka, Toblerone and Ritz — brands with genuine emotional attachment in their home markets. Roughly 40% of revenue comes from emerging markets, giving it more growth exposure than most packaged food companies but also more currency volatility.

Investment case

  • Iconic brands with strong local heritage — Cadbury in the UK, Milka in Germany, Oreo globally.
  • Emerging market exposure provides volume growth that developed-market food companies lack.
  • Snacking is one of the more resilient food categories through economic cycles.

Key risks

  • Cocoa prices reached record highs, severely compressing chocolate margins.
  • GLP-1 weight-loss drugs are a genuine long-term question for snacking volumes.
  • Private label competition has intensified as consumers seek value.
