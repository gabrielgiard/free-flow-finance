About Allianz SE

Founded in Berlin in 1890 and Europe's largest insurer by market value. Oliver Bäte has been CEO since 2015. Allianz sells property-casualty, life and health insurance in more than 70 countries and owns two of the world's largest asset managers, PIMCO and Allianz Global Investors. Priced in euros from Frankfurt, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • Diversification across insurance and asset management smooths earnings.
  • Return on equity in the mid to high teens.
  • Large dividends plus regular buybacks.

Key risks

  • Catastrophe losses and claims inflation in motor and property.
  • PIMCO earns less if bond markets see outflows.
  • German and European growth is slow.
