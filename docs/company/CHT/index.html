About Chunghwa Telecom Co., Ltd.

Spun out of Taiwan's telecom regulator in 1996 and still partly state-owned. Chunghwa is Taiwan's largest telecom company, with mobile, broadband and data-centre services, and dominant positions in fixed lines and fibre. Its steady business and generous dividend make it one of the most defensive stocks in Taiwan. One US-listed share represents ten Taipei shares.

Investment case

  • A near-monopoly in fixed broadband.
  • Very low debt and a high payout.
  • Data-centre services grow with AI demand in Taiwan.

Key risks

  • Little growth.
  • Mobile competition.
  • Taiwan geopolitical risk.
