About Emerson Electric Co.

Founded in 1890 as Emerson Electric Manufacturing, originally making electric motors and fans. Lal Karsanbhai has been CEO since 2021 and has reshaped the company into a pure-play automation business, divesting the climate technologies unit and acquiring control of National Instruments and Aspen Technology. Emerson sells the control systems, measurement instruments and software that run industrial processes in refineries, chemical plants and factories.

Investment case

  • Automation demand is supported by reshoring, labour scarcity and energy transition capital spending.
  • Installed base of control systems generates recurring aftermarket and service revenue.
  • The portfolio reshaping has meaningfully raised the quality of the earnings mix.

Key risks

  • Process automation spending follows the capital cycles of energy and chemicals customers.
  • Integrating AspenTech and National Instruments carries execution risk.
  • Competes against Siemens, ABB, Schneider and Honeywell, all formidable.
