About Constellation Energy Corporation

Spun off from Exelon in February 2022. Joe Dominguez has been chief executive since the separation. Constellation owns the largest fleet of nuclear power plants in the United States, which run around the clock and emit no carbon, making them highly sought after by technology companies powering AI data centres. It has signed long-term deals with Microsoft, including restarting a reactor at Three Mile Island, and with Meta. It completed the acquisition of Calpine, the largest US gas-fired generator, in January 2026.

Investment case

  • Carbon-free, always-on nuclear power is exactly what data centres want.
  • Long-term contracts with technology companies at premium prices.
  • Calpine adds scale and flexible gas generation.

Key risks

  • Much of the value depends on power prices and data centre demand staying high.
  • Regulators and grid operators may limit direct connections to data centres.
  • Nuclear plants carry operational and regulatory risk.
