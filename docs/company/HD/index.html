About The Home Depot, Inc.

Founded in 1978 by Bernie Marcus and Arthur Blank after both were fired from a hardware chain. Ted Decker is CEO, headquartered in Atlanta. Home Depot operates roughly 2,350 large-format home improvement stores across the US, Canada and Mexico. About half of sales go to professional contractors rather than consumers, and the acquisitions of SRS Distribution and GMS pushed it deeper into supplying roofing, landscaping and building products directly to trade customers, a market far larger than retail DIY.

Investment case

  • The professional contractor business is larger, stickier and less cyclical than consumer DIY.
  • Housing stock in the US is old, and ageing homes require maintenance regardless of transaction volumes.
  • Scale in distribution creates a cost advantage over regional suppliers.

Key risks

  • Mortgage rates above 6% have frozen existing home sales, and home improvement spending follows moves.
  • Large discretionary projects are deferred first when consumer confidence weakens.
  • Debt taken on for the SRS and GMS acquisitions is more expensive in a higher-rate environment.
