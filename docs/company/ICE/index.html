About Intercontinental Exchange, Inc.

Founded in 2000 by Jeffrey Sprecher, who bought a small Atlanta power exchange for one dollar and built it into a group that owns the New York Stock Exchange. Sprecher still runs it. ICE operates exchanges and clearing houses for energy, commodities and equities, sells fixed income data and analytics, and owns a large mortgage technology business built through the Ellie Mae and Black Knight acquisitions. In July 2026 it agreed to buy MarketAxess for about $5.7bn in cash, with closing expected in the first half of 2027.

Investment case

  • Exchanges and clearing houses are natural monopolies with regulatory moats.
  • Trading volumes rise with volatility, making the business partly counter-cyclical.
  • Recurring data and analytics subscription revenue smooths transaction volatility.

Key risks

  • Mortgage technology revenue is tied to origination volumes, which collapse when rates rise.
  • Heavy debt from the Black Knight acquisition, with further debt to be issued to fund the roughly $5.7bn MarketAxess purchase.
  • Regulatory scrutiny of exchange market data pricing is persistent, and the MarketAxess deal is under US antitrust review.
