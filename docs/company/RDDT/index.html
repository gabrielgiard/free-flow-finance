About Reddit, Inc.

Founded in 2005 by Steve Huffman and Alexis Ohanian, two University of Virginia graduates. Huffman returned as chief executive in 2015. Reddit hosts more than 100,000 active communities where people discuss everything from investing to video games, moderated by volunteers. It listed in March 2024. Most revenue comes from advertising, and it also licenses its conversation data to AI companies, including Google and OpenAI. Its pages increasingly appear in Google search results, which has driven fast user growth.

Investment case

  • Human conversation data is increasingly valuable for search and AI training.
  • Advertising revenue is growing fast from a low base compared with its audience size.
  • Very high gross margins and strong cash generation since listing.

Key risks

  • Much of its traffic comes from Google search, which it does not control.
  • AI answers could reduce clicks through to Reddit pages.
  • Volunteer moderators and communities can revolt against company decisions.
