About The Charles Schwab Corporation

Founded in 1971 by Charles Schwab as a discount brokerage, undercutting the fixed commissions that prevailed before deregulation. Rick Wurster became CEO in January 2025, succeeding Walt Bettinger. Schwab is the largest US retail brokerage with several trillion dollars in client assets, and earns money three ways: net interest on client cash, asset management fees, and trading revenue. The 2020 TD Ameritrade acquisition consolidated the industry substantially.

Investment case

  • Enormous scale in retail brokerage creates cost advantages competitors cannot match.
  • Client assets grow with markets and with net new accounts, giving natural operating leverage.
  • Cash sorting pressure from the rate cycle has largely worked through.

Key risks

  • Earnings are highly sensitive to interest rates through the client cash balance.
  • Clients moving cash to higher-yielding alternatives compresses the most profitable revenue line.
  • Zero-commission trading removed a revenue stream permanently and increased reliance on net interest.
