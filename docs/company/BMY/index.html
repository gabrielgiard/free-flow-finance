About Bristol-Myers Squibb Company

Traces to Bristol-Myers, founded 1887, merged with Squibb in 1989. Christopher Boerner became CEO in late 2023. Bristol Myers Squibb sells Eliquis, the leading blood thinner, and Opdivo, a foundational cancer immunotherapy. Both face loss of exclusivity within a few years, and the company has made a series of large acquisitions — Karuna, RayzeBio, Mirati — to rebuild the pipeline. Cobenfy, a novel schizophrenia treatment from Karuna, is the most important new launch.

Investment case

  • Cobenfy is the first genuinely new mechanism in schizophrenia in decades and addresses a large underserved population.
  • Trades at one of the lowest multiples in large-cap pharma with a substantial dividend.
  • Strong current cash generation funds both the dividend and continued business development.

Key risks

  • Eliquis and Opdivo together represent a very large share of revenue and both face exclusivity loss.
  • Recent acquisitions have been expensive and have not yet demonstrated returns.
  • US drug pricing reform disproportionately affects the highest-revenue products.
