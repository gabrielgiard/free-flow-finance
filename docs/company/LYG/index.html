About Lloyds Banking Group plc

Founded in Birmingham in 1765 and today the UK's largest retail bank, owning the Lloyds, Halifax, Bank of Scotland and Scottish Widows brands. Charlie Nunn has been CEO since 2021. It is the biggest UK mortgage lender with about a fifth of the market and holds a fifth of UK current accounts, so it is close to a pure play on the British consumer and housing market. One US-listed share represents four London shares.

Investment case

  • A 'structural hedge' of fixed-rate investments keeps lifting income as old low-yield positions roll off.
  • Unmatched scale in UK deposits gives it cheap funding.
  • Steady buybacks and a rising dividend.

Key risks

  • The UK motor finance commission scandal has cost billions in provisions and could cost more.
  • Almost entirely exposed to one economy and its housing market.
  • Political risk: windfall or bank taxes are a recurring idea in Westminster.
