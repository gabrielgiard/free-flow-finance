About United Parcel Service, Inc.

Founded in 1907 in Seattle as a messenger service by 19-year-old Jim Casey. Carol Tomé, previously Home Depot's finance chief, became CEO in 2020. UPS operates one of the largest package delivery networks in the world, with its own airline and a vast ground fleet. Its strategy under Tomé has been 'better not bigger' — deliberately shedding low-margin volume, including deliberately reducing its Amazon business, in favour of higher-yielding healthcare and small business shipments.

Investment case

  • An integrated global network that would cost an enormous amount to replicate.
  • Healthcare logistics is a high-margin, high-growth segment where UPS is investing heavily.
  • Deliberately reducing Amazon volume improves the average margin per package.

Key risks

  • Volume declines have been steeper than the margin improvement has offset.
  • The 2023 Teamsters contract substantially raised the cost base ahead of the volume recovery.
  • Amazon's own logistics network keeps expanding and competes directly.
