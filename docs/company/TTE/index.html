About TotalEnergies SE

Founded in 1924 as Compagnie française des pétroles at the direction of the French government. Patrick Pouyanné has been CEO since 2014. TotalEnergies is the European major that committed most seriously to an integrated power strategy — it owns and operates a large renewable generation and electricity retail business alongside conventional oil and gas. Its LNG portfolio is second only to Shell's, and its upstream assets are spread across Africa, the Middle East, the North Sea and Brazil. It has significant exposure to Mozambique LNG, a project delayed for years by regional security problems.

Investment case

  • Integrated Power gives a credible energy transition strategy that most peers abandoned.
  • One of the highest dividend yields among the majors, paid consistently through the cycle.
  • LNG position is strategically valuable in a Europe permanently reducing Russian gas dependence.

Key risks

  • Mozambique LNG remains delayed and the capital deployed is not yet generating returns.
  • African upstream assets carry political and security risk.
  • European majors trade at persistent discounts and French tax policy is unpredictable.
