About América Movil, S.A.B. de C.V.

Formed in 2000 when Carlos Slim's Telmex spun off its wireless business. The Slim family controls it; Daniel Hajj Aboumrad has been chief executive since 2000. América Movil is the largest telecom operator in Latin America, with Telcel in Mexico and Claro across most of the region, serving hundreds of millions of mobile customers. It also owns a large stake in Telekom Austria and fixed-line networks across the region.

Investment case

  • Market leader in Mexico with scale no rival can match.
  • Steady cash flow from essential mobile and broadband services.
  • Investment in fibre and 5G is past its peak, which frees up cash.

Key risks

  • Latin American currencies make dollar results volatile.
  • Mexican regulation of dominant operators limits pricing.
  • Telecom growth is slow and competitive.
