About Manulife Financial Corporation

Founded in 1887 as The Manufacturers Life Insurance Company, with Canada's first prime minister, Sir John A. Macdonald, as its president. Phil Witherington became chief executive in May 2025. Manulife sells life and health insurance, retirement plans and investment funds in Canada, across Asia, and in the United States under the John Hancock name. Asia, where insurance is still under-bought, is its largest source of new business.

Investment case

  • A leading insurer in Hong Kong, Japan, mainland China and south-east Asia.
  • Has shed older, riskier policies through reinsurance, freeing capital.
  • Rising dividends and buybacks.

Key risks

  • Old US long-term-care policies remain a potential source of losses.
  • Sensitive to interest rates and stock markets.
  • Economic or political trouble in China and Hong Kong.
