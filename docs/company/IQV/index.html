About IQVIA Holdings Inc.

Formed by the 2016 merger of IMS Health and Quintiles. Ari Bousbib has been CEO since. IQVIA runs clinical trials for pharmaceutical companies as a contract research organisation, and separately sells the prescription and healthcare data the industry uses for commercial decisions. Combining trial execution with data ownership is unusual and hard to replicate.

Investment case

  • Combining trial execution with proprietary healthcare data is a genuinely differentiated position.
  • A large committed backlog gives multi-year revenue visibility.
  • Drug development activity grows structurally regardless of which drugs succeed.

Key risks

  • Biotech funding weakness reduces the volume of trials being started.
  • Large pharmaceutical clients have been consolidating vendor spend and pushing on price.
  • Meaningful debt load from the merger and subsequent acquisitions.
