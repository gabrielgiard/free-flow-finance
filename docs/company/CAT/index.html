About Caterpillar Inc.

Formed in 1925 from the merger of Holt Manufacturing and C. L. Best Tractor, both pioneers of track-type tractors. Joe Creed became CEO in 2025, headquartered in Irving, Texas. Caterpillar makes construction and mining equipment, diesel and natural gas engines, and — increasingly important — the large reciprocating engines and turbines from its Solar Turbines unit that provide backup and prime power for data centres. Cat Financial provides equipment financing. The dealer network, largely independent and family-owned, is a major competitive advantage.

Investment case

  • Data centre power generation demand has become a genuine new growth driver for the Energy & Transportation segment.
  • The independent dealer network provides service coverage competitors cannot replicate.
  • Aftermarket parts and service revenue smooths the equipment sales cycle.

Key risks

  • Construction equipment demand is cyclical and directly tied to interest rates and non-residential building.
  • Mining capital spending depends on commodity prices that have been volatile.
  • The stock ran from $391 to $1,065 in twelve months, which leaves little margin for a cyclical downturn.
