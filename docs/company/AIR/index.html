About Airbus SE

Created in 1970 as a European consortium to compete with Boeing, and since 2019 the world's largest maker of passenger jets by deliveries. Guillaume Faury has been CEO since 2019. The A320neo family and the A350 make up most sales, alongside helicopters and defence and space. Its order backlog stretches more than ten years. Priced in euros from Paris, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • A backlog of more than 8,000 aircraft gives a decade of visibility.
  • Boeing's problems have strengthened its position.
  • European defence spending lifts its defence business.

Key risks

  • Supply chain problems, especially engines, keep delaying deliveries.
  • Space business losses have recurred.
  • Trade tensions and tariffs on aircraft.
