About Amphenol Corporation

Founded in 1932 by Arthur Schmitt in Chicago as American Phenolic Corporation. Adam Norwitt has been chief executive since 2009 and also became chairman in 2026. Amphenol makes connectors, cables and sensors — the physical links that carry power and data inside everything from fighter jets to cars to AI servers. Its decentralised model of dozens of small, entrepreneurial business units and a steady stream of acquisitions has made it one of the most consistent compounders in industry. It bought CommScope's cable and connectivity business for about $10.5 billion in January 2026.

Investment case

  • High-speed copper and fibre interconnect for AI data centres has become a major growth driver.
  • Exceptionally diversified across end markets and customers, which smooths the cycle.
  • A long, disciplined acquisition record that keeps adding growth.

Key risks

  • Much of the recent growth depends on AI data centre spending continuing.
  • Integrating the CommScope business is its largest acquisition to date.
  • Industrial and automotive markets are cyclical.
