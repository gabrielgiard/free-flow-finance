About British American Tobacco p.l.c.

Formed in 1902 as a joint venture between Britain's Imperial Tobacco and the American Tobacco Company. Tadeu Marroco has been chief executive since 2023. BAT sells cigarettes such as Lucky Strike, Dunhill and, since buying Reynolds American in 2017, Newport and Camel in the United States. Like its rivals it is trying to move smokers to alternatives: Vuse vapes, Velo nicotine pouches and glo heated tobacco.

Investment case

  • Cigarettes produce very large and predictable cash flow.
  • Velo nicotine pouches are growing quickly, including in the US.
  • A high dividend, with buybacks restarted.

Key risks

  • Cigarette volumes fall every year.
  • Illegal disposable vapes have undercut Vuse in the United States.
  • Regulation, litigation and a large debt load.
