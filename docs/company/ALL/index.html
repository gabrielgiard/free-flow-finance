About The Allstate Corporation

Founded in 1931 by Sears, Roebuck to sell car insurance through its catalogue, and spun off in 1995. Tom Wilson has been chief executive since 2007. Allstate is one of the largest US auto and home insurers, selling through its own agents, independent agents and directly online. It raised prices sharply in 2023 and 2024 to restore auto profitability and has sold its benefits and group health businesses to focus on property and casualty.

Investment case

  • Auto insurance profitability recovered strongly after large price increases.
  • A simpler company after selling its benefits businesses.
  • Investment income has risen with interest rates.

Key risks

  • Homeowners insurance is exposed to severe weather and wildfires.
  • Auto price competition is returning as profits recover across the industry.
  • Repair and medical costs keep rising.
