About The Toronto-Dominion Bank

Traces its history to the Bank of Toronto, founded in 1855, which merged with the Dominion Bank in 1955. Raymond Chun became chief executive in February 2025. TD is Canada's second-largest bank and has a large branch network along the US East Coast. In 2024 it pleaded guilty to failing to prevent money laundering in the United States, paid about $3 billion in penalties and accepted a cap on the size of its US retail bank, which it is now working to have lifted.

Investment case

  • A dominant, highly profitable Canadian retail franchise.
  • Excess capital after selling its Charles Schwab stake funds large buybacks.
  • A new management team is cutting costs and simplifying the bank.

Key risks

  • The US asset cap limits growth until regulators are satisfied.
  • Compliance spending is elevated for years.
  • Canadian households carry high mortgage debt.
