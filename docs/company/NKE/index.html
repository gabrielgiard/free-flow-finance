About NIKE, Inc.

Founded in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports by Phil Knight and his former track coach Bill Bowerman, renamed Nike in 1971. Elliott Hill, a 32-year Nike veteran who had retired, returned as CEO in October 2024 to reverse a strategic misstep: the previous management had pulled back from wholesale retail partners to push direct-to-consumer, ceding shelf space to competitors. Nike remains the largest athletic footwear and apparel brand in the world by a wide margin.

Investment case

  • The strongest brand in athletic apparel, built over six decades of athlete endorsement and marketing.
  • Rebuilding wholesale relationships restores access to shelf space Nike gave up.
  • Innovation pipeline and sport franchises give repeatable product cycles.

Key risks

  • On Running, Hoka and New Balance have taken meaningful share in running, Nike's heartland.
  • China revenue has been persistently weak and the recovery keeps being pushed out.
  • Excess inventory required heavy discounting, which damaged gross margin and brand positioning.
