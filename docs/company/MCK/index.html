About McKesson Corporation

Founded in 1833 in New York as a wholesale drug importer, making it one of the oldest companies in American healthcare. Brian Tyler has been CEO since 2019. McKesson distributes pharmaceuticals to pharmacies, hospitals and physician practices, moving an enormous volume of product on very thin margins. Its oncology business, which supports community cancer practices, is the higher-margin growth engine.

Investment case

  • Distribution is an oligopoly of three, with scale barriers that make new entry impractical.
  • Oncology and specialty distribution carry better margins than commodity drug distribution.
  • GLP-1 drug volumes have driven substantial revenue growth through the distribution network.

Key risks

  • Operating margins are around 1%, so small disruptions have outsized effects.
  • Opioid litigation settlements represent a multi-billion dollar obligation over many years.
  • Revenue growth is driven by drug prices and volumes it does not control.
