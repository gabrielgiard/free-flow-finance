About Starbucks Corporation

Founded in 1971 in Pike Place Market selling coffee beans; Howard Schultz transformed it into a coffeehouse chain after a trip to Milan. Brian Niccol, who had turned around Chipotle, became CEO in September 2024 with a mandate to fix deteriorating store throughput and a diluted brand experience. The 'Back to Starbucks' plan focuses on simplifying the menu, improving service times and restoring the cafe environment that mobile ordering had eroded.

Investment case

  • One of the most recognised consumer brands globally, with pricing power that has held through inflation.
  • The rewards programme has tens of millions of active members and drives repeat visits.
  • Niccol's Chipotle turnaround is a directly relevant and successful precedent.

Key risks

  • The turnaround is expensive: labour investment of at least $500 million and store renovations left the fiscal Q3 2026 operating margin at 12.9%, against 15.8% two years earlier.
  • China has been particularly weak, with aggressive low-price local competition from Luckin.
  • Unionisation efforts across US stores create ongoing labour cost and reputational pressure.

Recent developments

What the street says

A turnaround that is now showing up in the numbers: global comparable sales rose 7.9% in fiscal Q3 2026, with North American transactions up 4.5%. The question has shifted from whether traffic returns to whether margins recover, with management targeting an operating margin of up to 15% by fiscal 2028.
