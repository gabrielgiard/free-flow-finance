About Prudential plc

Founded in London in 1848 but, after spinning off its UK and US businesses, now a pure life and health insurer in Asia and Africa, run from Hong Kong. Anil Wadhwani has been CEO since 2023. It sells protection and savings policies across Hong Kong, mainland China, Singapore, Indonesia, Malaysia and India, largely through agents and bank partners. One US-listed share represents two London shares.

Investment case

  • Under-insured, fast-growing Asian middle classes are a long growth runway.
  • New business profits growing at double digits.
  • Buybacks and a rising dividend since 2024.

Key risks

  • Heavy dependence on mainland Chinese visitors buying policies in Hong Kong.
  • Execution misses in the past have hurt credibility.
  • Asian currencies and interest rates swing reported results.
