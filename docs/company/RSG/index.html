About Republic Services, Inc.

Formed in 1996 and built into the second-largest rubbish and recycling company in the United States, behind Waste Management. Jon Vander Ark has been chief executive since 2021. Republic collects waste from homes and businesses and owns the landfills where it ends up, which are extremely hard for a competitor to replicate. It has added hazardous-waste services, plastics recycling plants and projects that turn landfill gas into fuel. Bill Gates's investment firm is its largest shareholder.

Investment case

  • Landfills are scarce, permitted assets that give lasting pricing power.
  • Prices rise faster than costs year after year.
  • Demand barely changes in a recession.

Key risks

  • Construction and industrial waste volumes follow the economy.
  • Recycled material prices are volatile.
  • The shares rarely look cheap.
