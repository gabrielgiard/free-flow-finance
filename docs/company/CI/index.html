About The Cigna Group

Formed in 1982 by the merger of Connecticut General and INA, with roots back to 1792. David Cordani has been CEO since 2009. The Cigna Group has two very different businesses: Evernorth, which contains Express Scripts, one of the three pharmacy benefit managers that negotiate drug prices for most Americans, and Cigna Healthcare, a commercial health insurer. Evernorth generates most of the profit and is where the political risk sits.

Investment case

  • Express Scripts is one of three pharmacy benefit managers controlling the vast majority of US prescription volume.
  • Exited Medicare Advantage, avoiding the utilisation problems that have hurt peers.
  • Specialty pharmacy is a large and structurally growing profit pool.

Key risks

  • Pharmacy benefit managers face intense bipartisan political scrutiny over drug pricing and rebate practices.
  • Legislation forcing rebate transparency or delinking would directly threaten the economics.
  • Commercial insurance is exposed to employment levels and medical cost trends.
