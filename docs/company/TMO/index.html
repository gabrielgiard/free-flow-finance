About Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc.

Formed by the 2006 merger of Thermo Electron, founded in 1956, and Fisher Scientific. Marc Casper has been CEO since 2009 and has made the company the largest life sciences supplier in the world through relentless acquisition. Thermo Fisher sells the instruments, reagents, consumables and contract manufacturing services that pharmaceutical companies, hospitals and academic labs depend on. It profits from drug development activity itself, regardless of which drugs succeed.

Investment case

  • Sells to the entire industry rather than betting on individual drugs — a picks-and-shovels position in biopharma.
  • Consumables and reagents are recurring revenue tied to installed instruments.
  • An exceptional acquisition track record, with a repeatable integration playbook.

Key risks

  • Pharmaceutical R&D budgets and biotech funding drive demand, and both have been under pressure.
  • China represents a meaningful share of revenue and has been weak.
  • The post-pandemic normalisation in lab spending has taken longer than expected.
