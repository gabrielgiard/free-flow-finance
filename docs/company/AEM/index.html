About Agnico Eagle Mines Limited

Founded in 1957. Ammar Al-Joundi has been chief executive since 2022, the year it merged with Kirkland Lake Gold. Agnico Eagle is one of the three largest gold miners in the world, producing well over three million ounces a year, and it deliberately concentrates on politically stable places: most of its output comes from Quebec, Ontario and Nunavut in Canada, with further mines in Australia, Finland and Mexico.

Investment case

  • Mines in safe jurisdictions, which few large gold producers can claim.
  • A record of meeting its targets and controlling costs.
  • Net cash, with record gold prices feeding straight into profit.

Key risks

  • Profits depend on the gold price, which is at historic highs.
  • Mining costs rise with inflation and labour shortages in northern Canada.
  • Growth requires building new mines.
