About Natera, Inc.

Founded in 2004 by Matthew Rabinowitz and Jonathan Sheena. Steve Chapman has been chief executive since 2019. Natera runs genetic tests on fragments of DNA found in a blood sample. Panorama screens pregnancies for chromosomal conditions, Signatera checks cancer patients after treatment for traces of tumour DNA that signal a relapse, and Prospera monitors organ transplants. Signatera volumes have been growing very quickly.

Investment case

  • The leader in tests that detect cancer recurrence from blood, a young and fast-growing market.
  • Insurance coverage keeps widening to more cancer types.
  • Has turned cash-flow positive as volumes scale.

Key risks

  • Revenue depends on what insurers and Medicare agree to pay.
  • Guardant, Exact Sciences and others are competing in cancer testing.
  • Patent disputes with rivals are ongoing.
