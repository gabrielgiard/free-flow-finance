About Air Products and Chemicals, Inc.

Founded in 1940 by Leonard Pool, who pioneered building air separation plants on customers' sites rather than shipping gas to them. Eduardo Menezes became CEO in 2025 after activist pressure led to leadership change. Air Products supplies industrial gases — oxygen, nitrogen, hydrogen — under long-term take-or-pay contracts, typically fifteen to twenty years, with plants built next to the customer. It is the direct US competitor to Air Liquide and Linde.

Investment case

  • On-site contracts run fifteen to twenty years with take-or-pay terms, producing exceptionally predictable cash flow.
  • Once a plant is built next to a customer's facility, switching supplier is effectively impossible.
  • Industrial gases is a global oligopoly of three, with rational pricing behaviour.

Key risks

  • Very large hydrogen project commitments, including NEOM, have uncertain returns and heavy capital requirements.
  • The previous management's capital allocation drew sustained activist criticism.
  • Higher interest rates raise the cost of the debt funding these long-dated projects.
