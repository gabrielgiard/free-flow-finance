About Arista Networks, Inc.

Founded in 2004 by Andy Bechtolsheim, David Cheriton and Ken Duda. Jayshree Ullal has been CEO since 2008 and built it into the leading supplier of high-speed data centre networking. Arista sells Ethernet switches running its EOS operating system, a single software image across the entire product line that is a genuine advantage over competitors' fragmented software. Its customers are concentrated among hyperscalers — Microsoft and Meta have historically been the largest — and AI clusters require enormous quantities of high-bandwidth switching to connect thousands of accelerators.

Investment case

  • AI clusters need vastly more networking per server than traditional data centres, so Arista's content per rack rises.
  • The single EOS software image is a real technical advantage that Cisco has never matched.
  • Gross margins above 60% are exceptional for networking hardware.

Key risks

  • Customer concentration among a handful of hyperscalers is severe, and they negotiate hard.
  • Those same customers are developing their own switching silicon and white-box alternatives.
  • Ethernet versus InfiniBand competition in AI fabrics is not fully settled, and NVIDIA controls the alternative.
