About The Bank of New York Mellon Corporation

Founded in 1784 by Alexander Hamilton as the Bank of New York, the oldest bank in the United States; it merged with Mellon Financial in 2007 and now brands itself BNY. Robin Vince has been chief executive since 2022. BNY is the world's largest custodian, holding and servicing tens of trillions of dollars of assets for investors, and is central to US Treasury market settlement. It also runs Pershing for brokers and an asset management arm.

Investment case

  • Scale in custody and settlement that would be almost impossible to replicate.
  • A turnaround under Robin Vince has lifted margins and returns.
  • Fee-based revenue is less sensitive to credit losses than traditional lending.

Key risks

  • Revenue depends partly on interest earned on client deposits.
  • Fee pressure in custody and asset management.
  • Technology and operational risk in critical market plumbing.
