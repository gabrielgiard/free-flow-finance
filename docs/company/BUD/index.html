About Anheuser-Busch InBev SA/NV

The world's largest brewer traces its roots to the Den Hoorn brewery in Leuven, recorded in 1366. Its modern form came from a series of mergers: Belgium's Interbrew and Brazil's AmBev in 2004, Anheuser-Busch in 2008 and SABMiller in 2016. Michel Doukeris has been chief executive since 2021. AB InBev brews roughly one in four beers sold worldwide, including Budweiser, Corona, Stella Artois and Michelob Ultra, and is strongest in Latin America and Africa.

Investment case

  • Unmatched scale and the highest profit margins in brewing.
  • Debt from the SABMiller deal has fallen enough to allow buybacks and higher dividends.
  • Leading positions in growing markets such as Mexico, Brazil and Colombia.

Key risks

  • Beer volumes are flat or falling in many countries as people drink less.
  • Still carries a large amount of debt.
  • Currency swings in Latin America and weak demand in China.
