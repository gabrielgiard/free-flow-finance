About Altria Group, Inc.

Traces to 1847 as Philip Morris in London; the US business separated from Philip Morris International in 2008 and renamed Altria. Billy Gifford has been CEO since 2020. Altria sells Marlboro and other cigarette brands exclusively in the United States, along with oral nicotine products including on! pouches and NJOY e-vapor. The business is in structural volume decline, offset by consistent price increases — a strategy that works until it does not.

Investment case

  • Extraordinary margins and free cash flow conversion, funding one of the highest dividend yields in the S&P 500.
  • Marlboro has held roughly 42% US cigarette share for decades, giving genuine pricing power.
  • Regulatory barriers make new entry into cigarettes effectively impossible.

Key risks

  • US cigarette volumes decline every year, and the rate of decline has been accelerating.
  • Illicit disposable vapes have taken share and enforcement has been ineffective.
  • Menthol ban proposals and nicotine reduction rules are existential regulatory risks.
