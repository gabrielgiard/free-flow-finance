About AppLovin Corporation

Founded in 2012 by Adam Foroughi, who still runs it. AppLovin began by helping mobile game developers acquire users, then built Axon, a machine learning advertising engine that predicts which users will spend money in an app. Axon proved good enough that the company has been divesting its own games studios to focus purely on the advertising platform, and is extending it to e-commerce advertisers outside gaming.

Investment case

  • Axon has demonstrably outperformed competing mobile ad engines on return on ad spend.
  • Extending beyond gaming into e-commerce advertising multiplies the addressable market.
  • Exceptional free cash flow margins for an advertising technology business.

Key risks

  • Heavily dependent on mobile gaming advertising budgets, which are discretionary.
  • Short-seller reports have questioned attribution methodology and data practices.
  • The stock has re-rated enormously, so expectations embedded in the price are demanding.
