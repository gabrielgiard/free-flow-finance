About monday.com Ltd.

Founded in Tel Aviv in 2012 by Roy Mann and Eran Zinman, who are co-CEOs. monday.com sells a no-code work management platform that teams use to plan projects, run CRM and handle IT service requests, sold mostly to small and mid-sized companies, increasingly to large enterprises.

Investment case

  • Over 25% growth with strong free cash flow.
  • New products sell into the same customers.
  • Net cash and no debt.

Key risks

  • Growth slowing as it gets larger.
  • AI tools may change how teams manage work.
  • Sales to small businesses are cyclical.
