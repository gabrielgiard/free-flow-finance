About Bank of Montreal

Canada's oldest bank, founded in Montreal in 1817. Darryl White has been CEO since 2017. BMO has the largest US presence of the Canadian banks after buying Bank of the West in 2023, making it a top-ten bank in the American Midwest and California, alongside its Canadian retail, wealth management and BMO Capital Markets businesses.

Investment case

  • The US franchise gives it growth beyond Canada's concentrated market.
  • Integration of Bank of the West should lift US returns as costs come out.
  • Long record of paying dividends, unbroken since 1829.

Key risks

  • US credit losses on commercial loans have run higher than peers.
  • Return on equity lags the best Canadian banks.
  • Canadian housing and consumer debt are high.
