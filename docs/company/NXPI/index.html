About NXP Semiconductors N.V.

Spun out of Philips in 2006, with roots going back to Philips Semiconductors in 1953. Rafael Sotomayor is CEO. NXP is one of the largest automotive chip suppliers, making the processors, radar sensors, secure elements and battery management systems that go into modern vehicles. It is Nasdaq-listed despite being Dutch-headquartered, which is why it appears in this coverage.

Investment case

  • Automotive semiconductor content per vehicle keeps rising regardless of how many cars are sold.
  • Radar and secure car access are high-barrier businesses with long design cycles.
  • Design wins lock in revenue for the life of a vehicle platform, typically 5–7 years.

Key risks

  • Automotive is more than half of revenue and grew only 12% year on year in the second quarter of 2026, well behind industrial and communications infrastructure, so softer car production would weigh heavily.
  • Chinese domestic chipmakers are moving up the value chain in automotive.
  • Heavier debt load than analog peers limits flexibility.
