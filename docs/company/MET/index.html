About MetLife, Inc.

Founded in 1868. Michel Khalaf has been CEO since 2019. MetLife is one of the largest US life insurers, selling group benefits through employers — life, dental, disability — plus retirement solutions and international operations concentrated in Asia and Latin America. Group benefits is genuinely attractive: employers renew annually and switching disrupts every employee.

Investment case

  • Group benefits has high retention and low capital intensity relative to individual life.
  • Higher interest rates improve investment income on a very large asset base.
  • Asian operations provide growth that US-only insurers lack.

Key risks

  • Life insurance liabilities extend for decades, making the balance sheet hard to assess externally.
  • Sensitive to credit losses in a large corporate bond portfolio.
  • Long-term care and legacy blocks carry reserve risk.
