About Mitsubishi UFJ Financial Group, Inc.

Formed in 2005 by merging Mitsubishi Tokyo Financial Group with UFJ Holdings; its oldest predecessor, Mitsubishi Bank, dates from 1880. Junichi Hanzawa, who previously ran its main banking unit, is group chief executive. MUFG is Japan's largest bank and one of the largest in the world by assets. It also owns more than a fifth of Morgan Stanley and controls banks in Thailand and Indonesia. After decades of near-zero interest rates in Japan, rising rates are lifting what it earns on loans.

Investment case

  • Higher Japanese interest rates feed almost directly into profit.
  • The Morgan Stanley stake contributes a large, growing share of earnings.
  • Record profits, rising dividends and regular buybacks.

Key risks

  • Japan's economy is slow-growing and its population is shrinking.
  • A rapid rise in rates would cut the value of its bond holdings.
  • Returns on equity remain below those of leading US banks.
