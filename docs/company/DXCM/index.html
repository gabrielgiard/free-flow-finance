About DexCom, Inc.

Founded in 1999. Jake Leach is president and chief executive. DexCom makes continuous glucose monitors — small sensors worn on the body that track blood sugar in real time and send it to a phone, replacing finger-prick testing. The market is expanding well beyond insulin-dependent diabetics into type 2 patients and general metabolic health monitoring.

Investment case

  • Continuous glucose monitoring is still substantially under-penetrated even among insulin users.
  • Expansion into type 2 diabetes multiplies the addressable population.
  • The consumables model produces highly recurring revenue per patient.

Key risks

  • Abbott's Libre competes directly and is priced more aggressively.
  • Reimbursement decisions by insurers determine how fast the market can expand.
  • A US sales force reorganisation caused a growth stumble that damaged credibility.
