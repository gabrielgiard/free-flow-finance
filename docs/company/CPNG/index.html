About Coupang, Inc.

Founded in 2010 by Bom Kim, who dropped out of Harvard Business School to start it. Kim remains chief executive; the holding company is based in Seattle while operations are run from Seoul. Coupang is South Korea's largest online retailer, known for Rocket Delivery: it owns its warehouses and delivery fleet and delivers most orders overnight or the same day. It has extended into food delivery (Coupang Eats), streaming (Coupang Play), Taiwan, and luxury fashion through the 2024 acquisition of Farfetch.

Investment case

  • Owns the end-to-end logistics that makes overnight delivery possible across Korea, which competitors cannot easily copy.
  • The paid WOW membership keeps customers inside the ecosystem and lifts how much they spend.
  • Margins are still thin and have room to rise as the logistics network fills up.

Key risks

  • A large customer data breach disclosed in late 2025 brought regulatory scrutiny and reputational damage in Korea.
  • Chinese platforms such as AliExpress and Temu are competing hard on price in Korea.
  • New ventures such as Taiwan and Farfetch still lose money.
