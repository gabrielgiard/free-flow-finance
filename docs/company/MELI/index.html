About MercadoLibre, Inc.

Founded in 1999 by Marcos Galperin while he was a student at Stanford, launched in Buenos Aires and now legally headquartered in Montevideo. Galperin handed the chief executive role to Ariel Szarfsztejn, who had run the commerce business, at the start of 2026 and became executive chairman. MercadoLibre is the largest e-commerce marketplace in Latin America, with its own logistics network (Mercado Envios) delivering most parcels within two days, and Mercado Pago, a payments and credit business that has grown into one of the region's largest digital banks. Brazil, Mexico and Argentina are its three main markets.

Investment case

  • The rare emerging-market platform that owns both the marketplace and the payments, logistics and credit underneath it.
  • E-commerce penetration in Latin America is still well below the US and China, so the runway is long.
  • Mercado Pago's credit book is growing quickly and earns high yields on data the marketplace already collects.

Key risks

  • Lending to consumers in volatile economies exposes it to credit losses when local conditions turn.
  • Currency swings in Argentina and Brazil can erase dollar growth that looks strong in local terms.
  • Competition from Shopee, Temu and Amazon in Brazil and Mexico forces heavy spending on free shipping.
