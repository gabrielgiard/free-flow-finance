About Alphabet Inc.

Google was founded in 1998 by Larry Page and Sergey Brin at Stanford; Alphabet was created as the holding company in 2015. Sundar Pichai runs it from Mountain View. Revenue is dominated by advertising — Search, YouTube and the ad network — with Google Cloud as the fast-growing second pillar and a portfolio of Other Bets including Waymo autonomous vehicles and Verily. Alphabet designs its own TPU AI accelerators, runs the Gemini model family, and owns Android and Chrome, which together control how most of the world reaches the internet.

Investment case

  • Search remains one of the best businesses ever built — high margin, recurring, and still growing at a double-digit rate.
  • Alphabet owns the full AI stack: TPUs, data centres, models and distribution. No one else has all four.
  • Waymo is a genuinely valuable option that carries almost no weight in the reported numbers.

Key risks

  • AI chat interfaces are the first credible threat to the search query in twenty years, and the monetisation per query is lower.
  • Ongoing antitrust remedies in the US and EU could force changes to default search placement deals.
  • Cloud is a strong third place behind AWS and Azure, and third place in an oligopoly is an uncomfortable position.
