About JD.com, Inc.

Founded in 1998 by Richard Liu as a shop selling magneto-optical drives in Beijing, moving online in 2004. Sandy Ran Xu has been chief executive since 2023; Liu remains chairman. Unlike Alibaba's marketplace model, JD buys and holds much of its own inventory and runs its own nationwide logistics, which made its name for fast delivery of electronics and appliances. It entered on-demand food delivery in 2025, setting off a costly subsidy war with Meituan and Alibaba.

Investment case

  • Owns China's most reliable first-party logistics network, a real advantage in electronics and appliances.
  • Very large net cash position relative to its market value.
  • Trade-in subsidies from Beijing have supported appliance and electronics sales.

Key risks

  • The 2025 entry into food delivery is burning cash in a three-way subsidy war.
  • Chinese consumer demand remains weak and price competition from PDD is intense.
  • US-China tensions weigh on the valuation of every US-listed Chinese company.
