About The Williams Companies, Inc.

Founded in 1908 by brothers Miller and David Williams as a pipeline construction business. Alan Armstrong has been CEO since 2011. Williams owns and operates natural gas infrastructure, most importantly Transco — the largest natural gas pipeline system in the United States, running from the Gulf Coast to New York. Revenue is largely fee-based on contracted capacity rather than commodity prices, which makes cash flow far steadier than a producer's.

Investment case

  • Transco is an irreplaceable asset — permitting a competing interstate pipeline is effectively impossible today.
  • Fee-based contracted revenue means limited direct commodity price exposure.
  • Data centre and LNG export demand are both driving structural natural gas growth.

Key risks

  • High leverage, characteristic of midstream but a real constraint on flexibility.
  • Pipeline permitting and environmental opposition can delay or block expansion projects.
  • Long-term energy transition risk to natural gas demand.
