About Merck & Co., Inc.

Founded in 1891 as the US subsidiary of the German chemical firm Merck, becoming independent after the First World War. Headquartered in Rahway, New Jersey; Robert Davis is CEO. The company is defined by Keytruda, the PD-1 checkpoint inhibitor that is the best-selling drug in the world and treats a growing list of cancers. Other products include Gardasil for HPV, the Januvia diabetes franchise, and a large animal health division. Merck's central challenge is the Keytruda patent cliff arriving in 2028.

Investment case

  • Keytruda continues to add indications, and a subcutaneous formulation can extend the franchise's effective life.
  • Animal health is a genuinely good, non-correlated business that the market largely ignores.
  • The valuation already discounts a sharp Keytruda cliff, which is why our model deliberately builds a decline year into the forecast.

Key risks

  • Keytruda is roughly half of revenue and loses exclusivity in 2028. This is one of the largest patent cliffs in industry history.
  • Gardasil demand in China collapsed and has not recovered.
  • The pipeline has not yet produced an asset of remotely comparable scale.
