About Freeport-McMoRan Inc.

Traces to 1912 as Freeport Sulphur. Kathleen Quirk became CEO in 2024. Freeport is one of the largest publicly traded copper producers in the world, with major operations in Indonesia at the Grasberg mine — one of the largest copper and gold deposits ever found — plus assets in Arizona and South America. Copper is the metal most directly leveraged to electrification: grids, electric vehicles and data centres all require enormous quantities.

Investment case

  • Copper demand is structurally supported by electrification, grid investment and data centre construction.
  • New copper supply is scarce — permitting and building a mine takes well over a decade.
  • Grasberg is a genuinely world-class, low-cost orebody.

Key risks

  • Earnings are driven by the copper price, which is cyclical and outside management control.
  • Substantial Indonesian operations carry political, permitting and resource nationalism risk.
  • Mining is capital-intensive with significant environmental and closure liabilities.
