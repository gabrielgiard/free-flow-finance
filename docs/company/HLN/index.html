About Haleon plc

Spun off from GSK in 2022 (GSK and Pfizer had combined their consumer health businesses in 2019). Brian McNamara has been CEO since the listing. Haleon is the world's largest pure consumer health company: Sensodyne and parodontax toothpaste, Panadol and Advil painkillers, Centrum vitamins, Voltaren gel and Theraflu. One US-listed share represents two London shares.

Investment case

  • Brands people trust and pay up for, especially Sensodyne.
  • Steady 4-6% organic growth with improving margins.
  • Paying down debt and buying back stock.

Key risks

  • Store brands compete on price in painkillers and vitamins.
  • Volume growth in North America has been weak.
  • Legacy litigation risk around Zantac and other products.
