About Interactive Brokers Group, Inc.

Founded in 1978 by Thomas Peterffy, a pioneer of electronic trading who remains chairman and largest shareholder. Milan Galik has been chief executive since 2019. Interactive Brokers is a global electronic broker with a very low-cost, highly automated platform, used by active traders, professionals and other brokers in some 200 countries. It earns commissions and, especially, interest on client cash and margin loans.

Investment case

  • The lowest-cost, most automated platform among large brokers.
  • Fast account growth, especially outside the US.
  • A very strong balance sheet and pre-tax margins above 70%.

Key risks

  • Net interest income falls if interest rates drop.
  • Trading volumes swing with market conditions.
  • Founder Thomas Peterffy still controls the company.
