About ANSYS, Inc.

Founded in 1970 by John Swanson to commercialise finite element analysis. Ajei Gopal has been CEO since 2017. ANSYS makes simulation software that lets engineers test designs virtually — how a component fails under stress, how air flows over a wing, how heat moves through a chip — before anything physical is built. It is used across aerospace, automotive, electronics and energy.

Investment case

  • Simulation replaces expensive physical prototyping, so the return on the software is easy to demonstrate.
  • Deeply embedded in engineering workflows with validated processes built around it.
  • Growing chip design complexity increases demand for multiphysics simulation.

Key risks

  • Customer engineering budgets are cyclical and tied to industrial capital spending.
  • Long sales cycles into large enterprises make quarterly results lumpy.
  • Consolidation in the design software space has raised competitive intensity.
