About Sun Life Financial Inc.

Founded in Montreal in 1865 and one of Canada's three big life insurers. Kevin Strain has been CEO since 2021. Sun Life sells group benefits and health insurance in Canada and the US, runs the asset managers MFS and SLC, and has a fast-growing Asian business. It has moved toward fee-earning, capital-light businesses.

Investment case

  • A capital-light mix lifts returns: underlying return on equity around 18%.
  • Asia gives faster growth than North American life insurance.
  • Steady buybacks and dividend growth.

Key risks

  • MFS has seen outflows as investors move to index funds.
  • US dental and stop-loss businesses have had pricing problems.
  • Equity market falls hit asset management fees.
