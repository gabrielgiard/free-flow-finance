About Ryanair Holdings plc

Founded in Ireland in 1984 and turned into Europe's largest airline by passengers by Michael O'Leary, CEO since 1994. Ryanair flies about 200 million passengers a year on a single aircraft type, the Boeing 737, with the lowest costs in Europe, making money on fares, bags, seats and other extras. One US-listed share represents two and a half Dublin shares.

Investment case

  • The lowest cost base in European aviation by a wide margin.
  • Aircraft shortages limit rivals' growth while Ryanair's orders come in.
  • Net cash and a strong balance sheet.

Key risks

  • Boeing delivery delays slow growth.
  • Fuel prices and European air taxes.
  • Fares fall when consumers tighten spending.
