About Nebius Group N.V.

Nebius is what remained of Yandex N.V. after the company sold all of its Russian businesses in 2024; Arkady Volozh, who co-founded Yandex, leads it as chief executive from Amsterdam. Nebius builds and rents out AI data centres full of NVIDIA graphics processors, competing with CoreWeave as a specialist cloud for training and running AI models. It signed a multi-year capacity deal with Microsoft in September 2025 worth up to about $19 billion, and a further deal with Meta. It also owns stakes in Toloka and ClickHouse and the self-driving business Avride.

Investment case

  • Large long-term contracts with Microsoft and Meta give revenue visibility.
  • Demand for AI computing capacity still exceeds supply.
  • An engineering team with decades of experience running large-scale infrastructure.

Key risks

  • Building data centres requires enormous capital, funded by equity and convertible debt.
  • Customer concentration in a few very large technology companies.
  • Graphics processors lose value quickly as new generations arrive.
