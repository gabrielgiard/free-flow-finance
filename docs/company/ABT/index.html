About Abbott Laboratories

Founded in 1888 by physician Wallace Abbott. Robert Ford has been CEO since 2020. Abbott spans four quite different businesses: medical devices, diagnostics, established pharmaceuticals sold mainly in emerging markets, and nutrition products including Similac and Ensure. Its most important growth asset is FreeStyle Libre, a continuous glucose monitor that has become the standard of care for diabetes management and is expanding into non-insulin-dependent users.

Investment case

  • FreeStyle Libre is a category leader in a large and still-underpenetrated diabetes market.
  • Genuine diversification across four businesses smooths the earnings profile.
  • Structural heart and electrophysiology device franchises are growing well above company average.

Key risks

  • COVID testing revenue has fully normalised, creating a multi-year comparison drag.
  • Ongoing litigation relating to preterm infant formula creates a tail risk that is hard to size.
  • Dexcom competes directly and aggressively in continuous glucose monitoring.
