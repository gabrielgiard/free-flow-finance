About Novartis AG

Created in 1996 from the merger of Ciba-Geigy and Sandoz, both Basel chemical and pharmaceutical houses with roots in the 1750s. Vasant Narasimhan is CEO. After spinning off the Sandoz generics business in 2023 and the Alcon eye care business in 2019, Novartis is a focused innovative medicines company. Key products include Entresto for heart failure, Cosentyx in immunology, Kisqali in breast cancer, Kesimpta for multiple sclerosis, and the radioligand therapy Pluvicto — a genuinely differentiated cancer treatment that delivers radiation directly to tumour cells.

Investment case

  • Radioligand therapy is a differentiated platform with manufacturing complexity that acts as a barrier to entry.
  • Post-spin-off, the portfolio is cleaner and margins have improved materially.
  • Swiss domicile and strong cash generation support a reliable, growing dividend.

Key risks

  • Entresto has begun losing exclusivity, removing a large revenue contributor.
  • Growth is mid single digits, which limits the multiple the market will pay.
  • Pipeline depends on a small number of high-value assets performing.
