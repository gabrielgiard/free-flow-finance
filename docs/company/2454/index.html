About MediaTek Inc.

Founded in 1997 as a spin-off from United Microelectronics and today one of the world's largest chip designers. Rick Tsai has been CEO since 2017. MediaTek's Dimensity processors power more Android phones than anyone's, and it designs chips for TVs, Wi-Fi, cars and, increasingly, custom AI accelerators for cloud companies such as Google. Priced in Taiwan dollars, converted to dollars.

Investment case

  • Custom AI chips for data centres could become a large new business.
  • Strong position in premium Android phones.
  • Net cash and a high dividend payout.
